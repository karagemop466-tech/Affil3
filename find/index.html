The fit check

Which of these three cooking appliances is yours?

Answer three questions. The check reads the numbers on the manufacturer pages cited by this site and tells you which row they point at, and why. It is not a ranking and not a review: nobody here has cooked in these machines.

What does a normal dinner look like for you?
How much counter will you give it, before the drawer opens?
Do you often cook for more than six?

Runs in your browser. Nothing is sent, stored or counted.

Answer the first two questions to see a match. A third answer only changes what happens when a batch has to be big.

The two thermometers are a different question

A probe is not chosen by capacity or counter space, so it is not in this check. It is chosen by whether you want a number now or a number while you are in another room: instant read or leave-in probe.

Where every number came from

Capacity, case size and basket count are read from the manufacturer product pages for each model string, and the Amazon listing is kept beside them as a second reading. Where the two disagree, the disagreement is on the product page and on Flagged for review, and this check does not average them.
