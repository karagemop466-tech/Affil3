About

Who is behind this

Affil3 is an independent kitchen buying guide. It is published by the Affil3 editorial team and it is written for people standing in a kitchen with a tape measure, not for people shopping for entertainment.

How products get here

Only products submitted by the site owner are listed. Nothing was added from our own research, and no brand paid for placement. If that changes, it will say so on the page where it happens.

What we actually do

We read each maker's specification page, record the values with the date and link, and put the units beside each other so the differences are visible. Where a maker is silent or contradicts itself, we leave the value out and log why on the flagged for review page.

What we do not do

  • We do not claim to have tested anything. There is no test kitchen.
  • We do not publish prices or star ratings S03.
  • We do not use manufacturer photography. The images are editorial illustrations and are labelled as such FLG-015.
  • We do not run trackers other than our own click counter, which stores no cookies and no personal data.

Corrections

If a number here is wrong, open an issue in the project repository. Corrections are made in the data file, re-verified against the source, and logged. Nothing gets quietly edited.

About the byline

Pages are published under a team byline, not a person's name, because there is no named editor yet. That is a weakness, and it is logged rather than papered over FLG-014.
