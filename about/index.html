About Affil3

Who writes it

Affil3 editorial team. No named author or credentials are listed yet (FLG-014). Named authors will appear once a real person is confirmed.

How products are chosen

Only the five products submitted by the site owner are listed. No product was added from our own research.

How facts are checked

Each fact on a product row carries a source link to an official page. If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, the cell stays blank and the row is flagged. We do not infer values.

Corrections

If something is wrong, open an issue in the project repository. Corrections are logged on the Flagged for review page.
