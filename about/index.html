About

How this site is made

Five products, one register, and a publishing gate that refuses to let an unsourced number through.

Who writes it

Affil3 editorial team. The pages are generated from a verified register by an automated build, and every page states its date. There is no named editor and no byline photograph, because inventing one is worse than admitting the gap FLG-014.

If that matters to you as a reader, it should. A person who signs their work can be asked questions. Until one does, treat this site as a well-sourced index rather than a trusted friend.

How products get here

Only the five links the site owner submitted are listed. No product was added from our own research, no product is ranked, and no product is called the best. Where two products sit in the same cluster, the comparison page shows what the listing titles say and nothing more.

How facts are checked

  1. A product fact is read from the Amazon page the submitted link resolves to — title, breadcrumb, product ID — and dated. It is recorded as an observation, not as the intended product.
  2. A policy or safety fact is read on the publisher's own page and cited by source ID. The source register lists the page, the date it was last checked and the strength of the check: full text, excerpt, or observation.
  3. An unconfirmed value stays blank. The intended ASIN, the official listing URL, the confirmation date and the confirmation source are blank for all five products, because no official source has confirmed them.

References: the source register · every open flag · every open question.

What we will not publish

  • Prices, star ratings or availability. Amazon allows these only when Amazon serves them or when they come through the Creators/PA API S02. A hand-copied price is wrong by the next morning.
  • Test claims we did not earn. No page says a product was tested, measured or cooked with.
  • Product photography. The illustrations are decorative. Product images require an API licence we do not have FLG-015.
  • Cloaked or redirected links. Every outbound link goes straight to Amazon, marked sponsored, with the tracking ID in the URL S02.

Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The full statement sits under the masthead on every page and again in the footer, and the reasons for its placement are on the compliance page. Nothing on this site implies Amazon endorses it.

Corrections

Errors are corrected in public and logged on Flagged for review, including the two product destinations that were recorded the wrong way round earlier and later fixed. If you find something wrong, open an issue in the project repository. Corrections are dated; nothing is quietly edited.

How this site is built

Static HTML generated from JSON registers with no runtime dependencies, published by GitHub Pages. Before anything is published, tools/verify.mjs runs more than two thousand checks: disclosure present, Amazon links sponsored and tagged, no prices, no ratings, every image present, every internal link resolving, every source ID existing, every page in the sitemap and every guide in the feed. If a check fails, the build fails.
