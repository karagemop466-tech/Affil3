S-25 Should the gate tell a product link apart from a citation? awaiting your choice

The gate requires every amazon.com hyperlink on the site to carry rel="sponsored". For the links that pay for this site that is exactly right, and it is what Google's guidance asks for. It is over-broad for a citation: linking to Amazon's own privacy notice or help pages and marking them as paid placements tells Google something false about them. This surfaced while writing /privacy/ in this session - the gate correctly refused the page, and the link was removed rather than mislabelled or the gate weakened.

  • A. Keep the blanket rule. Any amazon.com link must be rel="sponsored", and pages cite Amazon URLs as plain text instead of linking them (current). current Tradeoff: Impossible to ship an untagged or unmarked product link, which is the failure that matters. Costs a few convenience links, and plain-text URLs are worse for readers on phones. · Effort: none · Risk: low
  • B. Make the rule precise and fail-closed: a link is product-intent if it targets /dp/ or carries tag= or data-outbound, and must then be sponsored AND tagged; every other amazon.com link must NOT be sponsored. Anything that cannot be classified fails the build. Tradeoff: Semantically correct against Google's guidance and it restores citation links. It is a change to a compliance gate, so it needs a negative test proving an untagged product link still fails, and it should not be made by a build session on its own authority. · Effort: 2 hours · Risk: medium
  • C. Keep the blanket rule for product links and allow an explicit allowlist of informational Amazon hosts and paths, checked literally. Tradeoff: Narrower than B and easier to audit - the allowlist is short and visible. It grows by hand and someone will eventually add a path they should not have. · Effort: 1 hour · Risk: low

Recommendation. B, with the negative test written first: break a product link's tag, confirm the build fails, then make the change. Do not adopt B without that test, because the blanket rule is currently the only thing standing between this site and an untagged outbound link.
