Suggestions for you to choose between. Nothing on this list is implemented until you pick an option. The site currently reflects the 'current' option in each row. Reply with the suggestion ID and option letter (for example 'S-03 B').

  • B. Hide all buy buttons until gate G-02 passes. Trade-off: Safer for compliance; the site has no paths to purchase until the owner tests.

Our recommendation: B, until G-02 passes.

S-02 Publishing cadence current: A

  • A. One new guide every two weeks (proposed default). Trade-off: Steady growth; needs a writing slot every two weeks.
  • B. One new guide per week. Trade-off: Faster growth; higher manual load.
  • C. A batch of 10 guides before launch, then monthly. Trade-off: Meets the 'at least 10 posts' rule of thumb (S11) sooner.

Our recommendation: C, to reach the 10-post rule of thumb before the Associates review.

S-03 Where the analytics collector runs current: D

  • A. Self-host tools/collector.mjs on a small VPS you control, behind HTTPS. Trade-off: Full control of raw data; you run the server.
  • B. Run the collector on a platform that supports Node.js with persistent storage. Trade-off: Less server work; check the platform's data terms.
  • C. Use a third-party privacy-focused analytics product. Trade-off: Quickest setup; a separate processor, so it needs its own disclosure.
  • D. No analytics for now (current). Trade-off: Nothing to maintain; no click or conversion data.

Our recommendation: A or B if you want measured queries before choosing the next topic.

S-04 Brand name current: A

  • A. Keep the working name 'Affil3' (current). Trade-off: Matches the repository.
  • B. Choose a consumer-facing name and domain. Trade-off: Better recall; needs a domain and a naming check.

Our recommendation: B before wider promotion.

S-05 Imagery current: A

  • A. Typography and layout only, no product photos (current). Trade-off: Avoids use of Program Content outside the Associates purpose (S02).
  • B. Original photography of products you own. Trade-off: Strong for reader trust; needs equipment and time.
  • C. Amazon-supplied images through the Associates tools. Trade-off: Allowed only as Program Content on your site; needs review against the IP License.

Our recommendation: B for guides; A for the front page.

S-06 Authorship current: A

  • A. Team byline 'Affil3 editorial team' (current). Trade-off: No invented person.
  • B. Named author with a real bio and a review date. Trade-off: Stronger signal of accountability; needs a real person.

Our recommendation: B once a real reviewer is named.

S-07 Content depth before Associates review current: A

  • A. Publish the 5 existing guides, then add more on the cadence chosen in S-02 (current). Trade-off: Fewer than the 10-post rule of thumb at launch (FLG-007).
  • B. Hold the site from review until 10 original posts exist. Trade-off: Aligns with S11; delays the tag-based program.

Our recommendation: B.

S-08 Seasonal content current: B

  • A. A gift and holiday-cooking guide hub for November and December 2026. Trade-off: Seasonal relevance; needs a first-party reason to rank it, and no trend data is in hand.

Our recommendation: B until measured queries (from S-03) show interest.
