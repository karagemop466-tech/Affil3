How the Journal works

Each article is written to answer a question or cover a trend a real buyer searches for, and to send the reader to a product page when the article is about a product we track. Every factual claim carries its source, and every product link is a disclosed, tagged Amazon affiliate link marked sponsored. No price, rating or review count is republished, and no hands-on test is claimed.

The verification record behind these articles lives on the Shop table, the source register, and the Flagged for review page.
