Eight quarts, one basket: the big-batch air fryer

For the batch that does not fit in two small drawers, one large basket is the simplest answer.

By ShopNow Kitchen editorial team · Published November 20, 2026 · Last reviewed November 20, 2026

The simplest air fryer is a drawer, a heating element and one basket. When you need more room than two small baskets offer but do not want a full oven, the single large-basket air fryer is the middle answer.

What an 8-quart basket is for

The unit we track is the Chefman TurboFry Touch 8 Qt air fryer, model RJ38-SQSS-8T. It holds 8 quarts (7.57 litres) in one basket, with a temperature range of 200 to 400 degrees F and a maker-published power figure of 1700 W. There is no divider to work around, which is the whole point: one big batch, one temperature, one basket.

  • A single basket means no divider and no second drawer to coordinate.
  • Eight quarts is enough for a large batch that would split awkwardly across two small baskets.
  • The basket and rack are top-rack dishwasher-safe, per the maker.

Read the wattage figure carefully

This is the one place our register found the maker and the retailer listing disagree. The manufacturer's page for RJ38-SQSS-8T states 1700 W; the Amazon listing for the same model number states a different wattage. We publish both readings side by side on the product page and flag the row, because a wattage figure you cannot attribute is not a figure to build a decision on.

There is a second, related point worth naming: a different 8-quart Chefman model, RJ38-8T-BLACK, carries a 450-degree Hi-Fry mode. The unit we track is the RJ38-SQSS-8T, whose published range tops out at 400 degrees F. Model numbers are the thing to match, not the quarts.

Questions readers ask next

Does 8 quarts mean it cooks more than a 6-quart dual basket?

In total volume, yes. But the two shapes suit different habits: one large basket for a single big batch, two baskets for two cooks at once.

Why does the wattage differ between pages?

The manufacturer page and the retailer listing for the same model number state different wattages. We flag it instead of choosing one, because we hold no authorized figure that settles it.
