When a basket isn't enough: the case for a 26-quart oven

When a drawer is the bottleneck, the answer is a door, a rack and a whole meal at once.

By ShopNow Kitchen editorial team · Published November 20, 2026 · Last reviewed November 20, 2026

Basket air fryers are excellent at the small stuff. The moment a dinner stops being one tray of fries and becomes a protein, a side and something on a second level, the drawer starts to feel small. That is the gap an oven-style air fryer was built to close.

What an oven-style fryer adds

The unit we track is the Emeril Lagasse 26 QT French Door Air Fryer Toaster Oven. It is a countertop convection cooker with french doors, racks and 26 quarts of interior room — the maker describes it as food for four to six people. The point is not that it is bigger. It is that a door and a rack let you run two levels at once and rotate trays halfway through, the way a real oven does.

  • Two or more levels of food at once, instead of one drawer.
  • A door you can open to check without losing a whole drawer of heat.
  • French doors, so you do not need the clearance a single hinged door swings through.
  • It doubles as a toaster oven, so it can hold counter space at breakfast too.

What it trades away

An oven-style fryer is a bigger object. The Emeril unit's case measures 16.9 by 15.8 by 14.8 inches as the maker lists them, and it needs oven-sized counter depth. If your counter is the constraint, this shape costs you more room than a basket fryer, and that is the trade to weigh honestly.

One honest note from our register: the number of one-touch presets is published differently by the retailer listing and the maker's own page. We publish both readings on the product page rather than picking the more flattering one.

Questions readers ask next

Is an oven-style fryer better than a basket fryer?

Not categorically. If you cook small batches and care about counter space, a basket fryer is the better fit. If you cook whole meals at once, the oven-style earns its footprint.

Why does counter depth matter more than capacity?

Because the case has to sit on the counter with the door open. A large capacity in a case that does not fit the space is not usable.
