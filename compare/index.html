Where the sources disagree

  • The Emeril 26-quart oven-style fryer is listed by Amazon with 24 presets and by Emeril's own page with 25. Both numbers are published on its product page and the row is flagged. FLG-023
  • The ThermoMaven listing does not name a model. The figures in its column are the manufacturer's published figures for the F1 Ace family, which matches the listing title feature for feature. They are labelled as family-page figures, not confirmed specifications of the listed unit. FLG-025
  • The Chefman 8-quart listing and its maker's page agree on the model number and the temperature range, but disagree on wattage (1000 W vs 1700 W) and case size. The maker's figures are published and both differences are named. FLG-026 FLG-027

How to read this table

Capacity is not a shared unit across these five: three are ovens with baskets, one is a handheld probe, and one is a wireless leave-in probe. The table compares what each maker publishes; it does not say which one to buy. For that, start with the question that matches your kitchen: the guides are written as questions, and each one names the products it covers.
