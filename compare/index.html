How to use this table

  1. Decide the shape first. Basket, French door or probe. The form decides how you load food and where the appliance lives.
  2. Then decide the size. Capacity is quoted differently across listings: total, per basket, or cavity. Read the manual before you trust a number here.
  3. Then check the things this table cannot show. Noise, preheat time, cleaning effort and how it fits under your cabinets. Those are in the manual and in your kitchen, not on Amazon.
  4. Then click through. Prices and availability are live on Amazon and out of date the moment we write them down, so we do not.
