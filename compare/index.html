Basket or oven-style: decide the shape before the brand

Everything else follows from this. Below is the difference in plain terms, then the two tracked appliances as worked examples of each shape.

Basket-styleOven-style
What it isA drawer with a fan above itA countertop oven with a door and racks
Cooks bestLoose food in one layer: fries, wings, vegetablesAnything that needs a rack, a tray or a whole bird
Biggest single itemLimited by the drawer: a small chicken at mostA whole chicken, a 12-inch pizza, a full tray of cookies
Two foods, two timingsTwo baskets or one basket and a pauseTwo racks, one shared temperature
Counter spaceSmaller footprint, drawer pulls outLarger footprint, doors open forward
CleaningBasket and crisper plate come outRacks, tray and a door with crevices
PreheatLittle to noneMore like an oven

This table describes the two shapes generally. It does not rank any product and is not a test result.
