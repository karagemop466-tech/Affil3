Reading this table without being fooled

  • Quarts are not comparable across shapes. A 6 qt figure split into two drawers is not the same cooking space as 6 qt in one basket, and a 26 qt oven measures the whole cavity around racks.
  • 'Not stated' outnumbers 'stated' more than you would expect. 0 of the 5 rows has no manufacturer page at all, so its entire column is blank on purpose.
  • Two official pages can disagree. Where the listing and the maker describe different machines, both readings are printed on that product's page rather than reconciled.

Which one fits which kitchen

Cooking for two, most nights

The dual-basket 6 qt: two small batches on two timers, in a footprint that fits under a cabinet.

A family, one big batch

The 8 qt single basket, after you confirm which model number you are ordering — that listing and the maker's page describe different machines.

Sides, roasts and a whole meal at once

The 26 qt oven-style unit: racks, a door instead of a drawer, and the counter depth an oven needs.

'Is it done yet?'

A thermometer answers that. They are not interchangeable: one reads a spot in seconds, the other watches a cook from the app.
