How accurate does a meat thermometer need to be, and what does 'accurate' even mean?

One probe and three probes. Every attribute below is quoted from the official listing title.

Two tools that answer the same question differently

Both of these exist to tell you when food is done. The single probe answers in one second while you stand there. The three-probe unit answers while you are somewhere else entirely, and answers about three things at once.

Neither is more accurate than the other according to the stated figures — both list a tolerance of ±0.5°F. The difference is how many cuts you are cooking and how much attention you want to give them. The reasoning is in the guide on which one you need.
