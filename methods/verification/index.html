How we verify

The rule this desk works to: a number that can be re-checked at the source may be printed. A number that cannot may not.

Every check run against these five products, and what it produced.
Check How it was checked Result
Link resolution Every submitted link was fetched and its redirect followed to the final amazon.com URL. Five of five resolved. The final URL, the page title and the browse-node breadcrumbs were read from the destination page.
ASIN shape Amazon defines an ASIN as a 10-character alphanumeric identifier, so the shape alone is checkable. Three tokens are 9 characters and cannot be ASINs. Two are 10 characters but their /dp/ pages did not return a retrievable listing, so neither was confirmed.
Listing titles The official page title is the product's own description, taken from Amazon rather than from us. Captured verbatim for all five products, each with its listing URL and the date it was read.
Browse path The breadcrumb on the listing page gives the category the product sits in. Captured for all five products: three under Air Fryers, two under Meat thermometers.
Prices and availability Checked against Program Policies 2(b) before deciding what to publish. Not published. The site links to the listing instead of quoting a figure.
Ratings and reviews Checked against Program Policies 5(t) before deciding what to publish. Not published, and deliberately absent from every comparison table.
Link format Checked against Program Policies 2(b) and 5(v) and 5(w). Every outbound product link is a direct Special Link carrying the Associates tag, with rel sponsored nofollow.
Disclosure placement Checked against Operating Agreement 5 and the FTC's Disclosures 101. A disclosure strip sits directly under the masthead on every page and the same sentence is repeated in the footer.
API status Checked the current API documentation rather than assuming the old one. PA-API 5 is deprecated and now returns HTTP 403 AccessDeniedException. The tooling targets the Creators API.

What is deliberately missing

Prices

Blank everywhere, on purpose. The rule and the source are in the source register.

Ratings and review counts

Absent from every table, on purpose, under a different clause of the same policy.

Internal dimensions and clearances

Not confirmed from any official source, so not published. They appear as questions to ask instead.

Popularity, trends and conversion

No such claim appears anywhere. There is no measured data behind one, and the campaign ledger is empty on purpose.

How a reader can check our work

Every row in every table on this site has a link in it. Click it. If the official page says something different from what we printed, that is a bug and we would like to know about it.
