Editorial standards

What we will and will not claim

A publication is defined by what it refuses to print. These are ours.

Updated 2026-11-20Corrections policy applies from launch

Authorship

Everything on this site is written and signed by the ShopNow Labs editorial desk. There is no bylined individual, because no individual has tested these products; inventing an author with a biography would imply an authority we do not have. Corrections and complaints are handled by the same desk.

What we have not done

We have not tested any product on this site. No unit was purchased, measured, or used. This is stated on the home page, in every guide's verification note, and here, because it is the single most important fact about the reliability of the recommendations.

What we did instead was read each product's Amazon listing on 2026-11-20 and record exactly what it says: capacity, stated temperature, modes named in the title, included accessories, stated warranty. Where a listing did not state something a buyer would want — interior dimensions, wattage, probe diameter — we say it is not stated rather than estimating it.

How specifications are handled

  • A specification is published only if it appears in the listing text we retrieved.
  • If two listings state different things about similar products, both are reported as stated, without reconciliation.
  • Marketing claims in a title — "sync finish", "4-in-1", "24 presets" — are reported as claims, in the listing's own terms, not as verified capabilities.
  • Review sentiment is reported only where Amazon's own review-summary widget displayed it, and is labelled as such. We do not convert anecdotes into failure rates.

Why there are no prices or star ratings

Amazon permits price and availability display only through its own served links or through its API, and requires that comparison prices show both the lowest new and lowest used price. We have no API access. Prices also change within hours, and star averages hide the distribution that matters. Both are omitted on purpose; the reasoning is on the known gaps page.

Imagery

No Amazon listing photography is used. Program Content may not be altered, and image caching is restricted. Our product cards use typography and a monogram instead. The page makes no third-party requests at all, which also means no tracking pixels load before you decide to allow them.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, it will be corrected and the correction will be noted on the page, not silently overwritten. The route is on the contact page. A correction that changes a recommendation will also be recorded on the source register with the date.

Independence

No brand paid for placement, and no brand reviewed a page before publication. The products covered on this desk were supplied by the site owner, which is itself a limitation on independence and is disclosed here rather than concealed.

AI assistance

Parts of this site's build — page templates, the sitemap, the feed, link validation — are automated. The prose was written and checked by the desk, and compliance statements are quoted from the sources listed on the source register. Amazon's policies prohibit using its Program Content to develop machine learning models without prior written approval, so listing text was not fed to a model to generate articles; that boundary is documented on the automation page and logged as FLG-009.
