Thermometers · P04

ThermoMaven 1 Sec Professional thermometer

A folding instant-read probe: a number in about a second, a magnet on the side of the fridge, and no app to open.

Best for: A quick spot check at the grill, not a leave-in monitor.
Trade-off: It reads one spot at a time, so it cannot watch a long smoke the way a leave-in probe can.
