CDF-01 high open link handling

The five supplied short links carry no tracking tag, so they cannot attribute a sale as supplied

All five supplied links are link.amazon short URLs. Followed on 2026-11-20, none of the destinations that resolved carried a tag= parameter. Program Policies 2(b) requires the Associates ID as a URL parameter in every link placed on a site (S02), and the Commission Income Statement 2(g) disqualifies purchases that are not correctly tracked because links are not properly formatted (S03). This edition therefore does not publish the short links as buy links. It publishes the observed product with the supplied tracking ID added, and labels each one provisional.

Why it matters: An untagged link earns nothing and, worse, produces a click record that cannot be reconciled against any report.

What would close it: Re-issue one link per product from Associates Central with tag=shopnow047c-20, click it, and confirm the click appears in the tracking report before the other four.

Sources: S01 S02 S03 S22

CDF-02 high open link handling

Two supplied links resolved differently within the same day

This pass followed all five links on 2026-11-20. Three resolved to a product detail page (B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DZFD6R9Z). Two — B04G1MEwY and B0c8moW59 — landed on the Amazon homepage with no product page. Earlier passes the same day recorded those two resolving to B0CDY277D3 and B0DHZTCNR5. A homepage landing and a checkpoint page are not evidence that a link is dead; they are evidence that the destination depends on session, region or timing. The observation is kept, dated, and never merged into the intent fields.

Why it matters: If the link the owner ships behaves like this in a real browser, readers will land on a homepage and leave.

What would close it: Open each of the five links in a signed-in browser, twice, and record the product page shown. That single step also answers which product each link was meant to reach.

Sources: S22 S09 S19

CDF-03 high open compliance

Compliance status of the supplied link.amazon short links is unresolved, not 'invalid'

Participation Requirements 6(w) restricts a link-shortening service used in a manner that makes it unclear that the link goes to an Amazon Site (S02). Whether these particular link.amazon URLs fall inside that restriction has not been determined by any document read here, and this edition does not claim they are invalid. They are recorded as untagged and unresolved. The same clause is why this site never wraps a product link in its own redirect: 6(v) treats a Redirecting Link as cloaking.

Why it matters: There is a real difference between 'this violates a rule' and 'no document says either way'. Only the second is true here.

What would close it: Check the supplied links against the tracking reports in Associates Central; if they are absent from the reports, stop using them as evidence of attribution.

Sources: S02 S03 S22

CDF-04 high open compliance

The tagged buy links have not been validated in Associates Central

Every outbound product link on this site is amazon.com/dp/<observed asin>?tag=shopnow047c-20, marked rel="sponsored noopener". That satisfies the formatting rule and the Google guidance (S04), and it is the format the Amazon-owned ASIN guide describes (S23). Formatting is not the same as validation: no link on this site has been clicked inside an enrolled Associates session and confirmed against a tracking report, because this build has no Associates login. Each buy button therefore says so in place.

Why it matters: This is the one gate between a page that links to Amazon and a page that earns anything.

What would close it: Run the one-link test in gate G-02, then mark the row validated with the date and the report it appeared in.

Sources: S02 S04 S23

CDF-05 high open product data

The 8 Qt Chefman listing and the 8 Qt Chefman product page state different wattage and different dimensions for the same model number

The listing for B08DKYBTPH says in its own 'About this item' copy: '1000 Watts/120 Volts – RJ38-SQSS-8T', with a 200–400 °F range and 12.75 × 13.5 × 10 in in the product details. Chefman's own product page for RJ38-SQSS-8T (8 Qt) states 1700 W / 120 V, 200–400 °F, and 15.4 × 12.6 × 13.5 in. Both are official pages; they disagree on wattage and on size, and they agree on model number, capacity and temperature range. This edition publishes capacity, temperature, weight, presets and cleanup, and withholds wattage and dimensions rather than picking a side. An earlier session concluded the listing described 'a different machine'; on reading the manufacturer page for RJ38-SQSS-8T that is too strong, and the correction is recorded as CDF-09.

Why it matters: Wattage decides whether a circuit is comfortable with the unit; footprint decides whether it fits. Both numbers are withheld rather than guessed.

What would close it: Ask Chefman support which wattage and dimensions ship under RJ38-SQSS-8T, or read the rating label on a unit.

Sources: S13 S14 S15

CDF-06 medium open product data

The Emeril 26 QT page disagrees with itself on presets and on what is in the box

One manufacturer page states '25 Cooking Pre-sets' in its specification strip and '24 one-touch cooking presets' in its description; the listing title says 24. The same page's description says 'Included are 7 premium accessories', the listing title says 'Six Cooking Accessories Included', and the manufacturer's own accessory page lists the grill plate as a separately sold part. So no preset count and no in-box list is published here.

Why it matters: Accessory lists decide whether a buyer needs a second purchase on day one.

What would close it: Ask the seller for the current box contents and the current preset count for 4-00675-02A-VN.

Sources: S11 S12

CDF-07 medium open product data

CHEF iQ states two different battery lives and two different sensor counts for the same product

The iQ Sense product page advertises '70+ Hours of Battery'. The same brand's FAQ states Gen 3 probes hold 'up to 60 hours'. The product page's feature list says '4 internal temperature sensors ... 1 ambient temperature sensor' while the page headline and the listing both say five sensors — which is consistent in total but reads as a contradiction at a glance. No battery figure is published here; the sensor split is published with the manufacturer's own wording.

Why it matters: Battery life is the difference between a leave-in probe that survives an overnight cook and one that does not.

What would close it: Ask CHEF iQ which figure applies to the three-probe Gen 3 bundle sold under B0DZFD6R9Z.

Sources: S16 S17 S18

CDF-10 medium open measurement

No analytics endpoint is deployed, so the live site records nothing

GitHub Pages serves static files and cannot run the collector. The five-event schema and a self-hostable collector ship in this repository, and the browser code fails open — it sends nothing when no endpoint is set. No click count, traffic figure or order number appears anywhere in this edition, because none exists. That is a limit, not an oversight.

Why it matters: Without an endpoint there is no way to tell a page that reads well from a page nobody opens.

What would close it: Choose a host for the collector, set the endpoint, and run a known set of ten test clicks through it before trusting a single number.

Sources: none — this is a gap in the record, not a disputed fact

CDF-11 medium open measurement

The campaign ledger is empty, and stays empty until an authorized report exists

The ledger schema in data/3b45b513/ledger.json defines nine columns and holds zero rows. Rows may only be added from authorized first-party reports (an Associates export the owner supplies, with its date range and provenance). No conversion rate, revenue forecast or traffic projection appears on this site. Nine of the ten columns that matter live in reports this build cannot reach.

Why it matters: A forecast built on no data is a fiction that gets believed six weeks later.

What would close it: Export the Associates report for a fixed window, supply it with its dates, and add one row per campaign window.

Sources: S03

CDF-12 low open discoverability

Search Console is not connected and reader capture is not built

The sitemap, RSS feed and robots.txt are generated and valid, but no Search Console property is verified because that needs a Google account this build does not have. There is also no email capture: the RSS feed is the only subscription route. Both are decisions with real trade-offs (privacy notice, consent, provider), not things to bolt on quietly.

Why it matters: Without a Search Console property, the next topic gets chosen from instinct rather than from impressions.

What would close it: Verify the URL-prefix property, submit /counter-desk/sitemap.xml, and read the Performance report after two weeks.

Sources: none — this is a gap in the record, not a disputed fact

CDF-13 low open editorial

The byline is a desk name, not a person

This edition is published under 'The Counter Desk editorial desk'. No named editor, credential or biography was supplied, and inventing a plausible-sounding person would be a fabrication of exactly the kind this project exists to avoid. A desk byline is the honest option; it is also a weaker trust signal, and it is logged as such.

Why it matters: Readers and search engines both read an accountable author as evidence that someone is answerable for corrections.

What would close it: Supply a name, a role and a contact route for the person who answers for corrections.

Sources: none — this is a gap in the record, not a disputed fact

CDF-08 medium resolved product data

The claim that no manufacturer page existed for the ThermoMaven thermometer was wrong

Earlier registers in this repository recorded that no manufacturer page could be found for the ThermoMaven instant-read, and withheld every specification as a result. That was a search failure, not a fact. This pass read the listing's item-model field (HT07, S19), matched it to the ThermoMaven F1 Stealth page and its user manual (S20, S21), and published the manufacturer's own measured range, probe size, battery, display, accuracy and warranty terms. The earlier 'no manufacturer source' flag remains visible in the repository's older registers as an audit trail; it is superseded here.

Why it matters: Withholding a product's specifications because of an incomplete search under-serves the reader as much as publishing a wrong number.

What would close it: None. The fields are filled from the manufacturer page and manual, both cited.

Sources: S19 S20 S21

CDF-09 medium resolved editorial record

A prior finding overstated a conflict: the listing names the model the maker also sells

An earlier pass published that the B08DKYBTPH listing and Chefman's page 'describe two different machines', because the listing names RJ38-SQSS-8T while the page read at the time was for RJ38-8T-BLACK. Reading Chefman's own product page for RJ38-SQSS-8T (S14) shows that model exists, is 8 quarts, and shares the listing's 200–400 °F range. The two pages still disagree on wattage and dimensions (CDF-05), but not on identity. The earlier flag is left in place as an audit trail; this row records the correction rather than overwriting it.

Why it matters: An overstated conflict is its own kind of error, and a correction log that quietly deletes the overstatement cannot be audited.

What would close it: None. Recorded so the next reader can see both versions.

Sources: S13 S14 S15

CDF-14 low resolved editorial

Imagery is illustration, and it says so on every image

The photographs on this edition are editorial illustrations generated for the publication. None of them is a photograph of the unit a reader would receive, and none carries a brand mark. Every figure is captioned as an illustration, and the disclosure line says the same thing in the footer. Amazon-hosted product imagery is Program Content and its use is limited by the IP License (S02), so it is not used here.

Why it matters: A stock photo standing in for a product is a small lie that makes every larger claim on the page cheaper.

What would close it: None. If real photography of the units is ever supplied, it replaces these and the caption changes.

Sources: S02
