What is built

  • A reader-facing publication: front page, a five-product shelf with a separate intent register, three category clusters, 7 question-led guides, an interactive counter-space checker, and an RSS feed with a sitemap.
  • Two disclosures above the fold on every page — the FTC's own example sentence and the Associates statement — plus a repeat in the footer S05 S01.
  • An operator layer: source register, product-intent register, flagged-for-review, open questions, suggestions with trade-offs, an empty campaign ledger, a measurement schema, and a rule-by-rule compliance record.
  • A build gate (tools/3b45b513/gate.mjs) that fails on a missing disclosure, an untagged or unqualified Amazon link, a price, a rating, a broken internal link, a missing image, an uncited specification, a sitemap gap or a feed gap.

What is verified

  • All five supplied links were followed on November 20, 2026. Three resolved to a product page; two resolved to the Amazon homepage. Both facts are recorded, dated, and flagged CDF-02.
  • Every published specification traces to a manufacturer page or a manufacturer manual, cited by id: Chefman twice, Emeril once, CHEF iQ twice, ThermoMaven twice S10 S12 S14 S17 S20 S21.
  • The Amazon rules that decide what this site may publish were read in full: the Operating Agreement, the Program Policies including the price, rating, cloaking and shortener clauses, and the Commission Income Statement S01 S02 S03.
  • The FTC's affiliate-disclosure guidance and 16 CFR 255.5 were read in full S05 S06, as were the USDA temperature chart and thermometer factsheet S07 S08, and Google's outbound-link guidance S04.

What is not verified, and why

  • Which product each link was meant to reach. Four intent fields per row are blank because no owner confirmation or Associates record was supplied CDF-02.
  • Whether a tagged link credits a sale. No link has been clicked inside an enrolled Associates session; there is no Associates login here CDF-04.
  • Wattage and dimensions of the 8 Qt Chefman. Two official pages disagree, so both are withheld with their figures recorded CDF-05.
  • Two battery-life and two preset-count figures. Each manufacturer contradicts itself or its own listing CDF-06 CDF-07.
  • Any traffic, click or order figure. The analytics endpoint is unset and the ledger is empty CDF-10 CDF-11.

Four decisions only the owner can make

  1. Confirm what each supplied link was meant to reach. One message closes the largest gap on the site.
  2. Run the one-link test in Associates Central. Formatting is not validation, and this is the gate between a page and an income.
  3. Decide where the analytics collector runs, or decide deliberately to run with none.
  4. Choose the canonical publication. This repository carries several independently generated editions over the same five links.

Each is written up with its trade-offs on the suggestions page, and each has an owner on the questions page.

What this build cannot do

It cannot read Amazon prices, sales or Associates reports, because that needs authorised API or account access the build does not have, and it does not scrape Amazon. Any figure that would require that data is absent on purpose, not forgotten. The honest position on revenue is that this publication earns nothing today, and will earn nothing until a tagged link is confirmed in a report — and that the first month of real data should be read, not scaled against.
