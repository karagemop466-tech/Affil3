Compliance

Disclosure, links and the gates before scaling

Every rule below cites the clause it comes from and names the thing in the build that enforces it. A rule with no enforcement mechanism is a wish, so none of them are listed without one.

The two statements and where they sit

Amazon's Operating Agreement §5 requires the site to state, clearly and prominently: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” S01

The FTC's affiliate-marketing guidance gives its own example wording — “I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post.” — and says the disclosure should sit as close to the recommendation as it can S05, which is what 16 CFR 255.5 means by clearly and conspicuously S06.

Both sentences appear in the disclosure bar directly under the masthead on every page of this site, and both are repeated in the footer. The bar is above the first product on the front page and above the first buy button on every product page, so no reader reaches a paid link without having passed it.
