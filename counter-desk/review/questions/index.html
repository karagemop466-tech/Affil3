Q-01 Which product did the owner intend each of the five supplied links to reach? Until this is answered, no row can say 'this is the product you submitted'. The site can only say 'this is where the link landed on this date', which is a different claim and a weaker one. Open each link in a signed-in browser and name the product, or supply the campaign brief and the link-generation record. One message closes it. Account holder yes S22
Q-02 Does a tagged buy link on this site actually credit a sale to shopnow047c-20? This is the only question that decides whether the publication earns anything. Matching the formatting rule is not the same as appearing in a tracking report. In Associates Central, click one link per product, confirm the destination, and confirm the click appears in the tracking report. That is gate G-02. Account holder yes S02 S03
Q-03 Is the Associates account active, and is this site declared in it? Associates tags must be embedded in links on the sites declared in the application. An account that has not declared the site, or has gone dormant, will not pay out however good the pages are. Check account status and the declared-sites list in Associates Central. Account holder yes S02
Q-04 Where will the analytics collector run, and who may read the data? GitHub Pages is static, so it cannot run the collector. Without an endpoint there is no measurement, and without measurement the next topic is chosen on instinct. Pick a host that runs Node or Python, deploy the collector, set the endpoint, and run ten known test clicks before trusting a number. Site owner no none
Q-05 Which marketplace is intended? Each marketplace has its own catalogue and its own Associates ID. The -20 tracking ID and the amazon.com pages point to the US programme, but that is an inference, not a statement from the owner. Confirm the US marketplace and the amazon.com storefront. Account holder no S23
Q-06 Who is the named editor? A named person with a contact route is a stronger trust signal for readers and crawlers than a desk name. Inventing one would be worse than not having one. Supply the name, role and contact route of the person who answers for corrections. Site owner no none
Q-07 What publishing cadence can actually be sustained? The feed and the sitemap only grow if guides keep shipping. A cadence nobody can hold is worse than a slower one that holds. Choose an option on the suggestions page and attach a first date. Editor no none
Q-08 Should prices ever be displayed, and through which authorised source? Displaying price requires Amazon-served data or the Creators API, and creates a maintenance obligation, since a stale price is a misleading claim. Decide whether to enrol in the Creators API. Until then, no price appears anywhere. Site owner no S02
Q-09 What belongs in the campaign ledger, and who exports it? The ledger stays empty until there is an authorised first-party report to put in it. Importing scraped or estimated numbers would poison every decision made from it. Export an Associates report, supply it with its date range and provenance, and add one row per campaign window. Account holder no S03
Q-10 Which of the repository's several publications should become the one canonical site? Several independently generated editions cover the same five links. They agree today because sessions keep checking by hand, which is not a mechanism. Choose one, write the redirect map, and retire the rest into the archive. Site owner no none
Q-11 Does the click counter need a privacy notice? The counter stores no cookies, no IP address and no identifiers, which keeps it small. Whether that is sufficient is a question for the owner's counsel, not a conclusion a build should state. Obtain a review, or leave the counter off until it is reviewed. Site owner no none
Q-12 Who owns the Search Console property, and when will the sitemap be submitted? Without it, no query data exists and nobody can see which guides are being found. Verify the URL-prefix property, submit /counter-desk/sitemap.xml, and review Performance after two weeks. Site owner no none
Q-13 Should readers be able to subscribe by email? Capture needs a provider, a privacy notice and a consent record. Asking for an address before there is anything to send teaches the first readers to ignore the next email. Choose A, B or C on suggestion SG-30. Site owner no none
Q-14 What is the reader, precisely? A site for 'people buying kitchen gear' is not a site. A site for 'renters with sixteen inches of counter cooking for two to four' produces different pages than 'families of six with a big kitchen'. Nominate one reader and one situation; every guide gets easier to write. Editor no none
