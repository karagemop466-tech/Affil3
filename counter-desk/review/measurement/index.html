The rules that shape the schema

  • No cookies, no local storage, no device identifiers, no IP address stored.
  • Five named events, and nothing else. The browser refuses to send an event outside the allowlist.
  • A click is not an order. This measures interest, not revenue.
  • If the endpoint is unset, the code fails open and sends nothing — the page must never depend on the network.
  • Outbound links are never routed through a redirect, because Program Policies 6(v) treats a Redirecting Link as cloaking and 6(w) bars a shortener that obscures the Amazon destination.

Not collected

IP addresses User agent strings Cookies or any persistent identifier Precise location Referrer URLs with query strings Amazon order, commission or customer data of any kind

Why an outbound click is never routed through this site

It would be easy to send the reader through a /go/<id> redirect and count the click on the way. Amazon's rules forbid it: Participation Requirements 6(v) treats a Redirecting Link as a way of obscuring the site a click came from, and 6(w) restricts link shortening that makes it unclear a link goes to Amazon S02. So the counter records the click in the browser and lets the browser follow the link directly. Fewer analytics, and a link that stays honest.

Why there is no chart on this page

A chart of nothing is a chart that gets screenshotted. When an endpoint is deployed and a thirty-day window exists, the numbers will appear here with their window, their source and their gaps — and not before.
