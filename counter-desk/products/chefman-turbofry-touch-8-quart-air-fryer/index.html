Who it suits, and what it costs you

Best for

One large batch of one thing, and a drawer wide enough for a long item.

The trade

No second basket and no window, so checking the food means opening the drawer and losing heat. Wattage is stated differently in two official places, so it is withheld (CDF-05).

Questions this page answers

Will a long item fit without cutting it?
The basket is single and undivided, which helps. Published dimensions conflict, so measure the drawer of the unit you receive before planning a long roast.
How much counter depth does it need?
Two official pages give 15.4 in and 12.75 in of depth for the same model number. Treat the larger figure as the safe planning number (CDF-05).
Do I have to learn a menu system?
No. Four presets plus manual time and temperature.
