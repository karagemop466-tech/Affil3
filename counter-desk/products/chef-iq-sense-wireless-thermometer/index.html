Who it suits, and what it costs you

Best for

A long cook where you would rather not stand next to the grill, and where more than one piece of meat matters.

The trade

The inserted portion of the probe tops out at 212 °F. The 1,000 °F figure is the ambient sensor, not something you can put in hot oil. It also needs the app and the hub in the loop.

Questions this page answers

Can I monitor from inside the house?
Yes. The hub relays over Wi-Fi rather than Bluetooth alone, which is what the unlimited-range claim rests on.
How many pieces of meat can I track at once?
Three with this bundle. The hub accepts up to four Gen 3 probes.
Will it survive a hot grill lid?
The ambient sensor is rated to 1,000 °F. The inserted portion is rated to 212 °F, which is a doneness range, not an oil-temperature range.
