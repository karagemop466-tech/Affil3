Who it suits, and what it costs you

Best for

Cooking two different foods that want different times, without heating the whole oven.

The trade

Each basket is 3 quarts, so a single big batch is split in two and a long item may not fit at all.

Questions this page answers

Will both baskets really finish together?
Sync Finish is the manufacturer's own claim; there is no independent test of how close the two baskets land.
Can I see the food without opening it?
Yes — each basket has a viewing window, and the interior light is stated on the manufacturer page.
Does it fit under a cabinet?
10.63 in tall before the lid opens. Measure your clearance rather than trusting a photo.
