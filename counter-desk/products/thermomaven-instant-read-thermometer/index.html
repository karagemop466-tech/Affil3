Who it suits, and what it costs you

Best for

Checking several pieces of meat at the grill in a few seconds each.

The trade

It is a spot-check tool. The manufacturer's own manual says not to leave it in the oven, the microwave or the dishwasher, so it is not a leave-in probe.

Questions this page answers

How fast is a reading?
About one second, per the manufacturer, using a thermocouple rather than a thermistor.
Can I wash it under the tap?
The manufacturer says the design is waterproof but publishes no IP number. Wash and wipe; the manual says not to put it in a dishwasher.
Will it live in the drawer or on the fridge?
Either. The manual describes a magnet on the body and a fold-shut probe that puts the unit to sleep.
