Who it suits, and what it costs you

Best for

Cooking several things at once for four to six people, and freeing the main oven during a holiday week.

The trade

It is the largest footprint in this set: 16.9 in deep and 14.8 in tall. On a shallow counter it will hang over the edge.

Questions this page answers

Does it replace a toaster oven and an air fryer?
Functionally yes by the manufacturer's own list; whether it toasts as evenly as a dedicated toaster oven is not something a specification sheet can answer.
Will a 12-inch pizza fit?
The manufacturer's description says it fits a 12-inch pizza; the interior dimensions to check that yourself are not published.
What is actually in the box?
The manufacturer's description says seven accessories; the listing title says six; the grill plate is sold separately on the manufacturer's own site. Ask the seller before ordering (CDF-06).
