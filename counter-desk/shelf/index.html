What the table will not tell you

Whether any of this food tastes good, how loud the fan is, or how evenly each machine browns. Nobody here has cooked in any of these appliances, and a specification sheet cannot answer those questions. What it can answer — capacity, footprint, temperature range, parts to wash — is in the table.

What a friend would ask first

If two foods, two timings

Chefman 6 Qt dual basket. Two independent 3-quart baskets and a Sync Finish setting, 13.38 inches deep.

If one big pile

Chefman 8 Qt single basket. Eight quarts in a single basket; wattage and size withheld because two official pages disagree.

If a whole tray

Emeril Lagasse 26 QT French Door. 26 quarts behind glass french doors, if you have 16.9 inches of counter depth.

If your hands are busy

CHEF iQ Sense, 3 probes. Three leave-in probes reporting over Wi-Fi while the lid stays shut.
