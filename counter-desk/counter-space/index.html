Counter space

Will it fit? Measure first, then choose

The most common reason a countertop appliance gets returned is that it does not fit where the buyer planned to put it. Three measurements solve that, and they take two minutes with a tape measure.

The four measurements that matter

  1. Depth. Wall to front edge. Published figures are the box, not the box plus the door.
  2. Height. Counter to the underside of the upper cabinets. Add clearance for anything that vents upward.
  3. Door swing or drawer pull. Measured toward you. This is the number that never appears on a listing.
  4. Outlet reach. Where the nearest socket is, and whether the cord reaches without an extension lead across a walkway.
Why two inches of clearance

A machine that fits exactly has no room for a vent, a hand on the handle, or a door that opens past ninety degrees. The finder adds two inches around every published dimension, so a “fits” here means fits with room to use it.
