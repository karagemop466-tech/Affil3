Three rules this site actually follows

A specification needs a manufacturer page

Capacity, wattage, dimensions and dishwasher claims come from the maker's own product page, fetched on the date shown next to the table. If the maker does not state it, the cell says “not published” and the reason is beside it.

No prices, no ratings, no exceptions

Amazon's program rules only permit a site to display price data that Amazon itself serves or that arrives through its API. Star ratings are a snapshot of a rolling average. We do neither, so nothing here goes stale in a fortnight.

When two official pages disagree, publish neither number

Two of the makers here contradict themselves or each other. Both readings are recorded on the flagged for review page with links, and the field stays blank.

The month the oven is fully booked

Late November, in the kitchen

For four weeks a year, counter space is the scarcest thing in the kitchen and the oven has a queue. That is the fortnight when a second cooking box and a probe that reports from inside the meat stop being gadgets and start being logistics.
