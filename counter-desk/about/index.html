About

How this publication works

A short, specific statement of method — written so that a reader can check us, and so that a future editor can be held to it.

Who publishes it

Counter Desk is published by The Counter Desk editorial desk. A team byline, deliberately. No named editor has been supplied, and inventing a person would be worse than admitting there is not one yet. If a named editor takes the byline later, the name, role and contact route will appear here and on every guide, and CDF-13 will be marked resolved.

How products get here

All five products were submitted by the site owner. Nothing was added from our own research, and no brand paid for placement. If that ever changes, it will say so on the page where it changes. There is no test kitchen behind this site and no hands-on testing of any appliance.

How a specification gets published

  1. Start with the Amazon listing the supplied link resolves to, and record its ASIN, title and breadcrumb with the date.
  2. Find the manufacturer's own product page for the model, using the listing's model-number field rather than its title. This is how the ThermoMaven page was found after an earlier pass wrongly recorded that none existed CDF-08.
  3. Read the specification block and publish only what it states, with the source id beside the value.
  4. If the manufacturer page and the listing disagree on a value, publish neither and write both down on the flagged page.
  5. If no official page states the value, the cell reads “not published” and gives the reason. It is never filled from a reseller, a review site or another model's page.

What we refuse to publish

  • Prices. Amazon's program rules permit a site to show price and availability only when Amazon serves that data or it arrives through the Creators or PA API S02. We do neither.
  • Star ratings and review counts. The same rules restrict republishing customer reviews and star ratings to API-sourced data, and a rating without its sample and date is a misleading claim S02.
  • Any claim that we tested something. No appliance on this site has been in our hands.
  • Manufacturer photography. Product imagery supplied through Amazon is Program Content and its use is limited by the IP License. Our images are editorial illustrations, captioned as illustrations, with no brand marks CDF-14.
  • Forecasts. No conversion rate, revenue projection or traffic estimate appears anywhere, because no data exists to support one.

How outbound links work

Every link to Amazon on this site points at a product page with the tracking ID as a URL parameter, and every one is marked rel="sponsored". Nothing is wrapped in a redirect: Amazon's rules treat a redirecting link as cloaking, and a shortener that obscures the destination is restricted too S02. The supplied short links are recorded as evidence in the register, not published as buy links.

Corrections

If a number here is wrong, open an issue in the project repository. Corrections are made in the data file, re-checked against the source, and logged on the flagged page — including corrections to our own earlier findings, of which there are two in this edition CDF-08 CDF-09.

What this site is not

It is not a test lab, not a price tracker and not a ranking. It is a shortlist with its working shown, which is useful for narrowing five appliances to one and useless for telling you how evenly any of them browns a chip.
