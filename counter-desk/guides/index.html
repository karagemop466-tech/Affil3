Why the order matters

Start with the shape guide, then the capacity checklist, then the cleanup guide. Those three decide whether you want a basket or an oven, how large a batch you actually cook, and how many parts you are willing to wash. The thermometer guides are a separate decision, and the seasonal one is a plan rather than a product question.
