How to decide in one minute

  1. Pick this if you want one appliance to replace a toaster oven and an air fryer.
  2. Pick a basket model if you mostly cook single portions; a 26-quart oven takes time to preheat for a handful of fries.
  3. Check the door swing: French doors open outward, so you need clear counter on both sides.
