How to decide in one minute

  1. Pick this if you cook long roasts and want a graph rather than a glance.
  2. Pick the instant-read if you only need a final temperature.
  3. Decide the probe count before you buy: it is sold in 1, 2, 3 and 4 probe sets.
  4. Check the internal range: the maker publishes 32–212 °F for the probe's internal sensor, which is cooking-temperature territory, not a high-heat searing claim.

Questions buyers ask

Does 1,000 °F mean I can sear with the probe in?

The 1,000 °F figure is the ambient rating. The maker publishes 32–212 °F for the internal sensor, so read the manual before using it over direct high heat.

S18

Do I need the hub?

Yes. The hub charges the probe and relays readings to your phone over Wi-Fi.

S18

Is a charging brick included?

No. The box has the probe, the hub and a USB-C cable.

S18

Can I put the probe in the dishwasher?

The maker says the probe is waterproof and dishwasher safe.
