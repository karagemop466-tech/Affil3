What the numbers mean in a real kitchen

Capacity in practice

Not a capacity product: it is a handheld probe.. A rated quart figure is the volume to the walls; usable space is always smaller, and it shrinks fastest with bulky or stacked food.

Cleaning

100% waterproof design, easy to clean (manufacturer F1 Ace page). No dishwasher statement.. Anything the maker does not call washable gets hand-washed; check the manual before the first wash rather than after.

Safety listing

NIST-certified ±0.5 °F accuracy (manufacturer F1 Ace page); the listing title also states NIST & NSF certified. A certification is a claim from the maker until you see it printed on the appliance's own label.

Warranty

Lifetime warranty, to be activated within 30 days of purchase (manufacturer F1 Ace page). Keep the order confirmation; a warranty claim without a dated proof of purchase is usually refused.
