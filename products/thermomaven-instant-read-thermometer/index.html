Best for

The trade-off

It reads one spot at a time, so it cannot watch a long smoke the way a leave-in probe can. The maker publishes no leave-in or IP rating.

Cleanup

Not stated as dishwasher-safe. The maker says '100% waterproof design' and 'rinse under water'; that is not the same claim.
