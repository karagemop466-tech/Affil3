Questions buyers ask

Can I leave it in the oven?

No. It is an instant-read probe for spot checks. The maker does not describe leave-in use.

S17

Is "NIST-certified" the same as a NIST certificate?

No. NIST is a standards body and does not certify consumer products. Treat the phrase as the maker's marketing claim until a certificate is produced.

S17

How do I clean it?

The maker describes a waterproof design that withstands spills and scrubbing. Wipe rather than immerse unless the manual says otherwise.

S17

What is the warranty?

The maker advertises a lifetime warranty that has to be activated within 30 days of purchase.
