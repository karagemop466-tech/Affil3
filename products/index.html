Why two tables

Amazon defines an ASIN as a 10-character alphanumeric identifier. Three of the five submitted tokens are 9 characters, so they cannot be ASINs. The other two are 10 characters but neither was confirmed as an issued ASIN. Merging the two facts would let a guess look like a verification, so they stay in separate tables. FLG-002 and FLG-004 →
