How to decide in one minute

  1. Pick this if your meals are usually a protein plus a side that need different times.
  2. Pick a single-basket model if you often fill the basket with one thing, because two 3-quart drawers will feel small.
  3. Measure the cabinet gap: the unit is 10.63 in tall and you need clearance to pull a drawer out.

Questions buyers ask

Is 6 quarts one big basket or two?

Two. It is two independent 3-quart drawers, so a single large item will not fit across both.

S14

Can I see the food without opening it?

Yes. Each basket has a window, and the unit has an interior light.

S14

How hot does it get?

450 °F at the top of a 200–450 °F range, per Chefman's specifications.

S14

Will it fit under my cabinets?

It stands 10.63 in tall and is 13.38 in deep. Measure the gap with the drawer open, not just closed.
