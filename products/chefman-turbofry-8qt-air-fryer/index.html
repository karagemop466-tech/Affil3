What it is

This is the appliance for a family that wants volume and does not want to manage two zones. It is not the answer for everyone on this desk, and the point of this page is to make that clear rather than to sell it.

The honest objection

A single basket means sequential cooking. If your meals involve a protein and a side finishing together, a dual-zone design suits that better.

If that objection is a dealbreaker for how you cook, no amount of convenience elsewhere will fix it. That is not a flaw in this review; it is the review.
