Twelve things nobody has answered yet

Blocking and high-priority questions

Was the fifth link meant to be submitted, and which product is it?

blocker site owner FLG-001
BlocksAny page, table row or recommendation for that product. Also any claim about the 'five products'.
We knowThe link resolves to a ThermoMaven digital meat thermometer (B0DHZTCNR5) when it is not intercepted, confirmed by two independent observations including a dated cross-session retest. Three of four requests from this environment were intercepted by an Amazon checkpoint page.
We don'tWhether the owner intended this product at all. It is not a countertop cooking appliance in the same sense as the other four, and it was never mentioned in the brief. Nothing is inferred from that.
To close itThe owner confirms the intended product, or sends the correct amazon.com/dp/ URL.

Are the supplied short links valid Associates links for this account?

blocker site owner FLG-002, FLG-003
BlocksDriving traffic. Attribution. The whole commercial premise.
We knowFour of the five resolved to real product pages. None of the four observed redirect targets contained a tag parameter. Amazon's policy requires the Associates ID to appear as a parameter in each link URL.
We don'tWhether attribution happens through a mechanism we cannot see from the redirect, and whether these links were generated in the Associates account for tag shopnow047c-20 at all.
To close itGenerate one link in SiteStripe for a known product, compare the resulting URL with the supplied short link, then click the supplied link once and look for a click in the Associates report.
