How these picks were made

We start from the five links you gave us. Each one resolves to an Amazon product page, and we read that page: the title, the breadcrumb, the product ID. That is the whole product dataset. Then we write about what to compare, not about which one wins.

Where a fact could not be confirmed from an official source — the intended ASIN, a price, a rating, a capacity we have not measured — the field is left blank and flagged. Blank is a result, not an oversight.

What this site does not do

  • No prices, star ratings or availability. Those change by the hour and belong on Amazon, not in a static page. FLG-016
  • No first-hand test claims. We have not cooked with these appliances, and we say so on every product page.
  • No cloaked or redirected links. Every outbound link goes straight to Amazon and is marked sponsored.
  • No email capture until there is a provider and a privacy notice that can honour it. FLG-014
