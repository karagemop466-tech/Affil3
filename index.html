Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Compare them on three things, in this order: how much usable counter depth you have, whether you ever cook for more than two people at once, and whether the second cooking zone is worth the cleaning. A basket air fryer is a fryer with a convection fan. An oven-style machine is a small oven that happens to blow air. Those are different products and they fail differently.
