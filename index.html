Why you can trust the numbers

Specs come from the maker, not the listing

Every figure on a product page was read on the manufacturer's own page and carries a source link. Where the Amazon listing disagrees with the maker, both are shown.

Nothing is guessed

If a manufacturer does not publish a value, the row says so instead of filling in a plausible number. Eleven specification rows are blank for exactly this reason.

No prices, no star ratings

Prices and ratings change hourly and Amazon's terms restrict how they may be shown. This site shows neither; it links you to the live listing instead.

Links are disclosed and sponsored

Outbound links carry our Associates tag and the sponsored attribute. The disclosure is at the top of every page, not buried in a footer.
