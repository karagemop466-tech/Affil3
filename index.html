How this shop works

Sourced or blank

Every row in every table on this site either links to the page it came from or says it is unconfirmed. Nothing is estimated, back-filled or averaged from a listing’s marketing copy.

No stale prices

Program policy permits a site to show price and availability only when Amazon serves it or a licensed feed supplies it. We have neither, so we show a link and the date we last checked instead of a number that decays.

Paid links say so

We earn from qualifying purchases at no cost to you. The note sits under the masthead on every page, again in the footer, and in the words above any product link.

Machine-checked

The registers are the source of truth and the pages are built from them. A build fails if a row loses its source, a disclosure goes missing, a product link loses its sponsored marker, or a price sneaks in.
