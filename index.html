What this site is, and is not

We did not test these appliances. There is no test kitchen behind this page. What we did is read the manufacturer's own specification sheet for each unit, record it with the date and the link, and put them beside each other so the differences are visible. Where a maker is silent, or contradicts itself, the cell stays empty and the reason is logged on the flagged for review page instead of being guessed.

That makes this a good place to shortlist and a bad place to decide alone. The specifications are checkable. The feel of a drawer, the noise of a fan, and the honesty of a non-stick coating are not, and nobody should pretend otherwise.
