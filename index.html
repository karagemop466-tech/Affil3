How we review

Three rules, applied to every page on this site.

1

Read the official page

Every product fact comes from the observed Amazon listing or another official source, linked on the spot. If a value cannot be confirmed, the cell stays blank and the row is flagged.

2

Say what we do not know

Unverified things are labeled unverified. Open questions live on the questions page; irregularities live on Flagged for review. Nothing is hidden in a footnote.

3

Disclose, then link

The disclosure is at the top of every page, and every Amazon link is marked sponsored. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, and we say so before you reach a single buy button.
