Disclosure, link rules and gates

Required statement

Operating Agreement §5 requires the Site to state, clearly and prominently: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” S01

The disclosure strip sits under the masthead on every page, and it is repeated in the footer. The FTC guidance asks that a disclosure be hard to miss and placed with the endorsement, not only on an About page or behind a “more” link S07, and the Endorsement Guides define clear and conspicuous placement S08.

Link rules applied

  • Every link to an Amazon host carries rel="sponsored noopener". Google recommends this for paid links and affiliate links S06.
  • Product links carry the tracking ID shopnow047c-20 as a tag parameter S02.
  • No redirect, cloaking or link-shortener is used for outbound links. The click counter does not route clicks S02.
  • The five submitted short links are shown only as source evidence, not as buy links (FLG-003).
