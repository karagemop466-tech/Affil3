Compliance

What this site does, which Amazon rule each thing comes from, and where the rules were read.

The disclosure

Amazon's Operating Agreement 5 requires this exact statement, clearly and prominently, wherever Program Content appears:

"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."

It appears in a strip directly under the masthead on every page of this site, and it is repeated in the footer. The FTC's Disclosures 101 says a disclosure is likely to be missed if it appears only on an About page or at the end of a post, which is why this one is at the top of the page as well.
