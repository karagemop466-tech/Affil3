Disclosure, links and gates

The required statement

Operating Agreement §5 requires the Site to state, clearly and prominently: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” S01

That sentence appears in the disclosure strip directly under the masthead on every page of this site, and again in the footer. The FTC's Endorsement Guides require a material connection to be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, and treat an affiliate commission as exactly that kind of connection; a disclosure buried on an About page is not enough S07 S08.

How outbound links are handled

  • Every link to an Amazon host carries rel="sponsored", which is what Google asks for on paid and affiliate links S06.
  • Buy links carry the tracking ID shopnow047c-20 as a tag parameter, which is the tagging rule in the Program Policies S02.
  • No redirect, no cloaking and no link shortener sits between the reader and Amazon. The click counter records the click in the browser and lets the link go straight out S02.
  • No price, star rating or availability figure is published, because Amazon's rules only permit that when Amazon serves the data or it arrives through its API S03.
