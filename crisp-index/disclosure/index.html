Disclosure & affiliate policy

Short version: we earn commissions from some links; you never pay more because of them; and every one of those links is labeled.

The required statement

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

This statement appears above the fold on every page of this site (the amber strip under the masthead), again in every footer, and on this page — per the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement, Section 5 S-01 · Operating Agreement and Associates help S-02 · Associates help.

What “Sponsored” means here

  • Every outbound product link carries a visible Sponsored chip and the rel="sponsored" attribute — the standard machine-readable signal for affiliate links.
  • If you buy through one of those links, we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases. The price you pay is identical either way.
  • Links to source documents (Amazon help pages, FTC guidance) are not affiliate links.

Why the disclosure sits up top

FTC guidance says material connections must be disclosed where people will see them — hard to miss, in plain language, with the endorsement itself — not buried on an about page S-04 · FTC Disclosures 101. The FTC’s Endorsement Guides further require that endorsements be truthful and not misleading S-05 · FTC Endorsement Guides. That’s why the amber strip is on every page and the chip is on every product button.

What we show — and don’t show — about products

  • Product names and category paths are quoted verbatim from the observed Amazon listings, each with its source link. Where a label is shortened for layout, the verbatim title is shown right beside it.
  • Prices and ratings are never displayed. They change constantly; we don’t cache them and we don’t verify them in real time. The buttons say “Check today’s price” because that’s the only honest price.
  • Product images on this site are original illustrations, not listing photos. We don’t hotlink Amazon imagery outside the approved tools.
  • Manufacturer claims (capacities, “dishwasher-safe,” feature names) are labeled as listing wording. They are not our test results.

Link compliance status

The submitted short links and their exact compliance status are unresolved and are tracked as FLG-003 — we do not claim they are invalid. Until each link passes the one-per-product Associates workflow test (destination + tracking report), all product URLs are marked provisional FLG-005. See the Flagged for Review register for details and actions.
