About

A small publication with a strict source rule

The Crisp Index is written and edited by The Crisp Index Editorial Desk — an editorial team identity, not a fictional persona. We publish buying guidance for countertop kitchens, and we fund it with clearly marked affiliate links. Those are the only two promises that matter: useful guidance, and an honest money story.

Methodology

  1. Register first. Every product enters the source register with its submitted link, intended identifier, and source date — before it appears anywhere reader-facing.
  2. Observe, then quote. We follow each submitted link once and record the destination in a separate field. Listing titles and category paths are quoted verbatim with source links. We never merge what a link says with where it goes.
  3. Blank beats invented. If a value can’t be confirmed from an official source, it stays blank and gets a flag. Prices and ratings are volatile, so we don’t cache them at all.
  4. Guides before verdicts. Our guides are decision frameworks and checklists. We publish “best of” verdicts only when a documented hands-on testing method exists — and today it doesn’t, so we don’t.

What we don’t do

  • No paid placements, ever. If a brand ever pays for coverage, it will be labeled as such in the same strip as our affiliate disclosure.
  • No invented testing. No fake lab coats, no stock-photo “our team”, no made-up personas.
  • No cached prices, no unverified ratings, no guessed specs.
  • No urgency theater. Guides stay up to date or get corrected.

Corrections policy

Found an error, a stale claim, or a listing that changed? Say so — every guide page links back here, and corrections are logged with the date, what changed, and why. Since our claim is “sourced,” being wrong is a bug with a paper trail.

How the site is funded

Commission income from qualifying purchases through links marked Sponsored — following the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and FTC disclosure guidance. Full detail lives on the disclosure page. Affiliate income never buys a ranking, a verdict, or a quieter flag.

Editorial roadmap

We publish on a two-guides-per-month cadence with seasonal anchors, and choose upcoming topics from measured search queries once Search Console is connected — not from instinct. The proposal queue (Suggestions & decisions) is where that gets decided in the open.
