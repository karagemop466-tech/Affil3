Observation method

  1. Each submitted link was fetched individually on 2026-11-20, following redirects.
  2. The final destination, its ASIN (read from the /dp/ URL), the page title, and the category breadcrumb were recorded verbatim as observations.
  3. Two fetch attempts were made for B0c8moW59; both returned Amazon’s “Continue shopping” checkpoint interstitial, so no destination was recorded for P-04 FLG-004.
  4. Prices and ratings seen in page carousels referred to other items and were not attributable to the resolved products; nothing was recorded FLG-010.
  5. The token-format check compares each submitted token against Amazon’s published ASIN definition (10-character codes, typically “B0” + 8) S-03. A mismatch is recorded as a checkable fact; validity is not claimed either way FLG-003.
