Executive summary

What this project is, what shipped, what is verified, what is flagged, and what needs a decision. Written for the owner reviewing after launch — same facts as EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md in the repository.

1 · The project in one paragraph

An Amazon affiliate publication — The Crisp Index — for countertop kitchen products, monetized with tracking ID shopnow047c-20. The reader-facing side is a shop-like magazine: question-shaped buyer’s guides, a comparison hub, and a test queue of the five submitted links. The machinery side keeps it honest: a source register, a flagged-for-review register, a suggestions queue, open questions, and an empty-but-defined campaign ledger. Every product link is marked Sponsored and every page carries the required Associates statement above the fold.

2 · Monetization & links

  • Five links submitted (tokens B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj). Intended identifiers recorded exactly as submitted; observed redirect targets recorded in separate fields and never merged.
  • 4 of 5 submitted links resolved to Amazon listing pages on 2026-11-20 (three air fryers, one probe thermometer); 1 hit Amazon’s checkpoint interstitial and stays unverified FLG-004.
  • Outbound URLs are provisional tagged links (?tag=shopnow047c-20 on observed listing URLs) pending the one-link-per-product Associates workflow test FLG-005. Per the brief, the short links’ compliance status is unresolved and is not claimed invalid FLG-003.
  • All five tokens are 9 characters and mixed case; Amazon’s own ASIN guide documents ASINs as 10-character codes FLG-001 S-03 — a format mismatch, flagged for review, not a validity claim.

3 · What shipped

  • Publication site (GitHub Pages): front page, 5 question-shaped guides, compare hub, test queue, about/methodology, disclosure page.
  • Compliance layer: disclosure strip on every page + footer repeat (exact Operating Agreement statement), Sponsored chips and rel="sponsored" on every product link, blank-and-flag rule for unconfirmed values.
  • Machinery: product register, verification log, flag register, suggestions queue, open-questions list, campaign ledger schema, privacy-preserving click counter (dry-run), sitemap, RSS feed (SG-28), robots.txt, 404 page, build + validation tooling.

4 · Verification posture

Every table row on data surfaces carries an official source link or an explicit “unverified — flagged” state. Sources fetched and cited 2026-11-20: the Associates Operating Agreement S-01, Associates disclosure help S-02, Amazon’s ASIN guide S-03, FTC Disclosures 101 S-04, FTC Endorsement hub S-05, and the four observed listing pages S-06 S-07 S-08 S-09. Prices, ratings, and review counts are blank everywhere FLG-010.

5 · Measurement (Priority 4 / 5)

The campaign ledger is empty and well-defined: rows appear only from authorized first-party reports, with a dated provenance trail. No conversion score or revenue forecast exists in this project. The click counter ships disabled because GitHub Pages cannot host an endpoint; reference servers ship in tools/analytics-endpoint/ FLG-006. The brief’s “five defined events” are not enumerated in the brief; a proposed five-event schema awaits confirmation FLG-009.

6 · Decisions needed from the owner

Fifteen suggestions sit in the decision queue (brand approval, cadence, analytics host, newsletter stack, price display policy, link-swap plan), and twelve open questions block specific next steps — chief among them: confirming what the five tokens intend to identify (Q-001), and the Associates account status for tag shopnow047c-20 (Q-007).

7 · Risks & flags

Eleven items are flagged for review; none block the site, and none are hidden from readers. The two that gate growth: link compliance testing (FLG-003/FLG-005) before adding any product, and the analytics endpoint (FLG-006) before any optimization work.

8 · Next actions (in order)

  1. Owner confirms intended products for the five tokens (Q-001) and Associates status (Q-007).
  2. Run the one-link-per-product workflow test; swap to validated Special Links (SUG-015).
  3. Deploy the analytics endpoint and confirm the five-event schema (SUG-003/SUG-004).
