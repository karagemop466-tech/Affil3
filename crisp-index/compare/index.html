The decision path

  1. Answer the shape question — what do you cook most? (guide 1)
  2. Measure twice — counter, cabinet height, and the largest meal. (guide 2)
  3. Price the invisible costs — cleanup parts and storage. (guide 3)
  4. Interrogate the feature list — replace vs add. (guide 4)
