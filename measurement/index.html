Privacy

No cookies. No IP address stored. No user-agent stored. No fingerprinting. No cross-site identifiers. Counts are aggregated per day, and browsers sending Do Not Track are not counted at all.

Not collected: cookies, IP address, user-agent, account data, anything typed into the page, cross-site identifiers.

Why clicks are not routed through a redirect

The Program Policies prohibit cloaking the URL of a site that carries Special Links, including by sending readers indirectly through an intermediate page. A redirect-based click counter is exactly that pattern, so the counter records the click in the browser and lets the link go straight to Amazon. S02

How a click actually earns

A qualifying purchase begins when a reader clicks a tagged link and ends at the first of: 24 hours, an order, or a click on somebody else's link. If the item goes into the cart first, the window extends to 89 days. S17
