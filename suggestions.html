Data • SUG-01

Resolve the five products before anything else is written

awaiting decision

The problem The submitted tokens and the pages they reach do not match. On 2026-11-20 four of five resolved to a live listing whose ASIN differs from the submitted token, and one resolved to an Amazon checkpoint page instead of a product. Three land in Air fryers and one in Meat thermometers, so the set is not even one category.

What to do Treat the product set as unknown. Ask for the ten-character ASIN of each intended listing, or a full amazon.com/dp/ URL, taken from the listing itself while signed in. Re-verify each one and record intended and observed values in separate columns of data/link-verification.json, which already has both fields.

What the reader notices A reader never sees a card titled "Product 1 - submitted listing". They see a named product with a named reason behind it.

Expected effect Unblocks every other content decision. Removes the single most audit-like element on the current page.

Effort S    Risk Low. The risk is in the opposite direction: publishing product claims built on a fallback destination.

Policy this rests on

  • CLM-02 — “For example, you must include your Associates ID or "tag" (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b) General Requirements Applicable to All Special Links, updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-05 — “You must not make inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or otherwise misleading claims about any Product, an Amazon Site, or any of our policies, promotions, or prices.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)

Needs answered first Q-01, Q-03

Paths

  • Owner supplies ten ASINs — Fastest and cleanest; one paste.
  • Promote the four observed listings instead — Zero input needed and each is a real product page, but you are then promoting whatever the fallback surfaced, not what you picked. Not recommended as a silent choice.
  • Rebuild the set from a category you choose deliberately — Best merchandising outcome, most owner input.

If approved, paste this into the build prompt

Populate data/link-verification.json intended_asin and intended_listing_url from this list of confirmed ASINs, re-run the resolver to confirm each destination, and only then rewrite the product cards with the confirmed names.

Compliance • SUG-02

Generate every live link as a tagged Special Link; keep the short links as evidence only

awaiting decision

The problem None of the five submitted links carries an Associates tag, and Program Policies require the tag as a URL parameter on every link placed on the site. An untagged or mis-tagged link earns nothing and can end the account.

What to do Build the site's outbound links as https://www.amazon.com/dp/ASIN?tag=XXXXX-20&linkCode=...&th=1 produced inside the enrolled workflow, one link per product. Keep the originally submitted short links on the verification page as dated evidence, clearly labelled as input, never as the click target.

What the reader notices Invisible to the reader, which is the point. Buttons read "Check price on Amazon" and land on the right listing.

Expected effect Attribution and therefore revenue start working. Also the only way to test whether a link is even eligible to earn.

Effort S    Risk Low. Do not hand-construct tags before the account is confirmed, since a wrong tag also fails to track.

Policy this rests on

  • CLM-01 — “The links must properly use the special "tagged" link formats we provide and comply with this Agreement ("Special Links").…” (Amazon Associates Program Operating Agreement, Section 1, updated 2025-10-15)
  • CLM-02 — “For example, you must include your Associates ID or "tag" (appearing as XXXXX-##, or such other format as we may designate) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b) General Requirements Applicable to All Special Links, updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-08 — “You must not encourage customers to bookmark your Special Links. All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)

Needs answered first Q-01, Q-02

Paths

  • Test one product first, confirm in the tracking report, then scale — Recommended and exactly what the brief asks for; one extra day.
  • Scale all five at once — Faster, but a systemic tag error then touches every page.

Add scripts/build_links.py that renders every outbound href from data/link-verification.json plus the confirmed Associates tag, refuses to emit a link without a tag parameter, and fails CI if any product href on the site does not match the register.

Storefront • SUG-03

Stop shipping prices and star ratings; ship a decision instead

safe to build now

The problem A shop page tempts you to hardcode the price and the star count you saw this morning. Program Policies allow price and availability only when Amazon serves the link or when the data comes from Creators or PA API. Neither applies here, and scraped prices go stale within days.

What to do Replace the price column with a what-to-compare block: capacity, footprint, power, controls, dishwasher-safe parts, warranty terms as stated on the listing, each linked to the listing as its source. Use "Check current price on Amazon" as the button, which is both compliant and what a confident shop looks like.

What the reader notices Reads like a specialist with a point of view rather than a mirror of a product page that will be wrong by Friday.

Expected effect Removes the most common reason affiliate sites get de-indexed and demonetised at once, and removes the staleness maintenance burden entirely.

Effort M    Risk Lower conversion on cold traffic that wants a number, partly recovered by the API path in SUG-04.

Policy this rests on

  • CLM-03 — “Product prices and availability may vary from time to time. Because prices for and availability of Products that you have listed on your Site may change, your Site may only show prices and availability if: (a) we serve the link in which that price and availability data are displayed, or (b) you obtain Product pricing and availability data via Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements regarding use of Creators API or PA API in the License.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-05 — “You must not make inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or otherwise misleading claims about any Product, an Amazon Site, or any of our policies, promotions, or prices.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-09 — “we may make available to you data, images, text, link formats, widgets, links, marketing content, and other linking tools, application program interfaces, Alexa functionality, and other information in connection with the Associates Program ("Program Content")…” (Associates Program Operating Agreement, Section 1, updated 2025-10-15)

Needs answered first nothing — you can decide on the evidence already on this page

Paths

  • No prices ever, criteria only — Safest, permanent.
  • No prices until API access, then API-served widgets — Recommended; keeps the door open without ever hand-typing a number.

Remove any numeric price or rating field from all product components. Add a compare-specs block whose rows each link to the listing, and a single CTA labelled "Check current price on Amazon".

Automation • SUG-04

Get product data from an API, not from page scraping

blocked

The problem The brief asks for automated product research, pricing, review and competition analysis. Everything about products on this site currently has to be hand-retrieved, which is slow, fragile, and the reason so many fields sit blank. The Program Content clause points at the sanctioned route: data and images supplied through Amazon's own tools and interfaces.

Content • SUG-05

Build category clusters with a weekly cadence, and write them like a person

awaiting decision

The problem The site currently has four guide pages and no editorial identity. A cluster needs a hub, several leaves, and internal links between them. Separately, Amazon's own eligibility rule says original content reusing third-party material must add significant commentary, analysis, or transformation, which is the formal version of the owner's objection to AI slop.

What to do One hub per cluster (dual-basket air fryers, air fryer ovens, counter-space and cleanup, multi-function appliances, cooking thermometers), each with four to six question-shaped leaves on a weekly cadence. Every leaf must contain something no listing says: a trade-off, a measurement method, a failure mode, or a decision rule. Voice rules to enforce it: second person, no throat-clearing openers, no "in today's fast-paced world", no sentence starting with "Whether you're", no paragraph that could be moved to another product unchanged, one concrete number or hands-on step per section, and read the page aloud once.

What the reader notices Feels like a specialist newsletter with a shop attached, which is the format that actually converts.

Expected effect Topical authority, internal linking, and the reason to rank that the brief asks for. The anti-slop rules are what make the difference between this and a content farm.

Effort L    Risk Cadence is a commitment. Publishing three strong guides then stopping beats twenty generated ones.

Policy this rests on

  • CLM-04 — “Special Links must link to Products (as defined in the Products Statement). When linking to pages with Product lists you must have additional original content on your Site that is relevant to the Special Link. Product lists include search results, events (e.g. Prime Day), or a department homepage (e.g. grocery).…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-05 — “You must not make inaccurate, overbroad, deceptive or otherwise misleading claims about any Product, an Amazon Site, or any of our policies, promotions, or prices.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-12 — “Your Site(s) must contain original content and be publicly available via the website address provided in the application. Original content utilizing third-party materials must contain significant commentary, analysis, or transformation to any materials you include.…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 1 Enrollment and Eligibility, updated 2026-04-14)

Needs answered first Q-05

Paths

  • Weekly, one cluster at a time — Recommended; sustainable and measurable.
  • Fortnightly with longer pieces — Better depth per URL, slower cluster completion.
  • Burst of ten at once, then pause — Looks published for a month, then signals abandonment.

If approved, paste this into the build prompt

Draft the cluster hub and leaves for <cluster name> using the voice rules in docs/STYLE.md; every factual claim about a listing must carry a listing link, and every page must add at least one trade-off or measurement step that the listing does not state.

Compliance • SUG-10

Move disclosure from a banner to a sentence the reader believes

safe to build now

The problem A single strip under the masthead satisfies the letter of the requirement and is otherwise ignored. FTC guidance is that the connection must be clear and conspicuous in the context where the endorsement is made, and staff guidance is explicitly not a safe harbour.

What to do Keep the strip above the fold and the footer repeat, and add a plain sentence inside each page next to the first product link, in the reader's language, saying what happens if they click and what it costs them. Include it in every guide, not only on the hub. Put the same sentence in the RSS description so a syndicated copy carries it too.

What the reader notices Said once, honestly, where it matters, and then out of the way. Confidence, not hedging.

Expected effect Meets the standard in context rather than in a corner, and reads as a publication that has nothing to hide.

Effort S    Risk Very low. The failure mode is burying it in a footer only.

Policy this rests on

  • CLM-16 — “if there's a connection between an endorser and the marketer that a significant minority of consumers wouldn't expect and it would affect how they evaluate the endorsement, that connection should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. ... Our staff guidance can't be definitive because the context of any particular endorsement is very important ... This guidance doesn't provide a safe harbor from potential liability…” (Federal Trade Commission, FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking, updated Guides revised in 2023, including a new definition of "clearly and conspicuously")

Needs answered first nothing — you can decide on the evidence already on this page

Paths

  • Strip plus per-page in-context line — Recommended.
  • Add a modal on first outbound click — Highest visibility, measurably annoying, hurts conversion.

If approved, paste this into the build prompt

Measurement • SUG-11

Measure clicks and conversions, but only in aggregate

awaiting decision

The problem The brief wants click and conversion measurement before more writing, and a self-hosted endpoint plus five defined events. Two constraints collide: GitHub Pages serves static files with no receiver, and Program Policies forbid tying any tag or sub-tag to a specific end user or dynamically assigning sub-tags to track visitor behaviour.

What to do Count events server-side in aggregate only: outbound_click per page and per destination ASIN, disclosure_impression, guide_view, product_impression, session_start as a per-day count. No cookies, no IP retained, no client-side identifier joined to a sub-tag. Ship a tiny receiver (one container or one edge function) that appends to a log, and point the existing beacon at it. Keep per-post sub-tags for channel attribution, since a sub-tag identifies the link, not the person.

What the reader notices Nothing on the page. Speed, and no consent banner creeping into the layout.

Expected effect Answers which cards get clicked and which guides precede a click, which is the input every later content decision needs.

Effort M    Risk Real hosting and retention decisions. Anything that profiles a visitor crosses CLM-07 and drags in consent obligations. Marked not-yet-operational until a receiver exists.

Policy this rests on

  • CLM-07 — “Under no circumstances may you associate any sub-tag, other ID or reporting provided in connection with the Associates Program with a specific end user of your Site (e.g., you may not dynamically assign sub-tags to users as they arrive on your Site for purposes of monitoring such users' behavior).…” (Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 2(b), updated 2026-04-14)
  • CLM-17 — “... bots or automated software, repetitive Bonus Events, and Bonus Events that do not result from Special Links on your Site ...…” (Associates Program Commission Income Statement (bonus-event exclusions; fragment as retrieved), updated 2026-04-14)

Needs answered first Q-09

Paths

  • Search Console only for now — Zero infrastructure, no click counts.
  • Self-hosted aggregate counter — Recommended once a host exists.
  • Third-party analytics — Easiest, weakest fit with a no-personal-data posture.

If approved, paste this into the build prompt

Deploy analytics/server.mjs behind a relative /collect path, log one JSON line per event with page, event, destination ASIN and day, and document the retention window in privacy.html.

Automation • SUG-16

Automate the re-verification sweep, not the writing

awaiting decision

The problem Product listings change: they get renamed, moved to a new ASIN, go unavailable, or the seller edits the specs. The site publishes on a dated snapshot, so a page silently becomes wrong. The brief asks for continuous optimisation; the honest first version of that is continuous checking.

What to do A scheduled job that re-fetches each linked listing, compares observed title, availability, and browse node against the stored snapshot, opens an issue on drift instead of editing pages, and never auto-publishes a replacement claim. Human approves; then the register updates.

What the reader notices Stale pages get caught, which is what "last checked" is supposed to mean.

Expected effect Converts verification from a launch-time ritual into a running property of the site, at the exact point where automation is safe.

Effort L    Risk Automated access to Amazon pages must stay polite and lawful; drift detection should alert, not rewrite. CLM-17 is the reason the write side stays human.
