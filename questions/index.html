Q-06Who is the named editor?A named person with a contact route is a stronger trust signal for both readers and search engines than a team byline. Inventing one would be worse than not having one.Supply the name and role of the person who answers for corrections.noS09
Q-07What publishing cadence can be sustained?The feed and the sitemap only grow if guides keep shipping. A cadence nobody can keep is worse than a slower one that holds.Choose an option on the suggestions page.nonone
Q-08Should prices ever be displayed?Displaying price requires Amazon-served data or the Creators API. It also creates a maintenance obligation, since a stale price is a misleading claim.Decide whether to enroll in the Creators API. Until then, no price appears anywhere.noS03
Q-09What belongs in the campaign ledger?The ledger stays empty until there is an authorized first-party report to put in it. Importing scraped or estimated numbers would poison every decision made from it.Export an Associates report and supply it with its date range and provenance.noS01
Q-10Should the earlier /editorial/ publication be retired?Two publications in one repository splits the site's identity and its search signals. One site should be indexed.Choose option S-01 on the suggestions page.noS09
Q-11Does the click counter need a separate privacy notice?The counter stores no cookies, no IP address and no identifiers, which keeps it small. Whether that is enough is a question for the owner's counsel, not a conclusion this build should state.Obtain legal review, or keep the counter off until it is reviewed.nonone
Q-12Who owns the Search Console property?Without it, no query data exists and nobody can see which guides are actually being found.Verify the property and submit the sitemap URL from robots.txt.nonone
Q-13What is the target reader, precisely?A site for 'people buying kitchen gear' is not a site. This decision changes the guides.Nominate one reader and one situation. Content decisions get easier immediately.nonone
