Q-01

What is the intended Amazon product (10-character ASIN or product URL) behind each of the five submitted links?

Blocks: the product-intent record

Without it, the intended ASIN and official listing URL cannot be filled, and the observed destination cannot be confirmed as the intended one.

How to close it Owner supplies the product URLs, or the owner opens each short link in a signed-in browser and confirms the product.

Is the Associates account active and does it declare this site (the GitHub Pages URL or a custom domain)?
