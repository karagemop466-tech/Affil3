## Editorial and design direction for a later approved implementation

1. Lead with a clear buyer problem, a short comparison path and truthful product cards; give each card an explicit reason to consider it, a trade-off and a cited source, not an invented winner.
2. Develop question-led pieces as hypotheses: basket versus oven style; usable capacity; cleanup and counter space; multifunction appliance trade-offs. Do not claim these are trending without data. Add each approved article to the preferred sitemap and RSS feed.
3. Use a consistent real brand and human editorial accountability. Use original or properly licensed imagery only; never imply hands-on testing or an individual author's credentials without evidence. [Google people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
4. Keep the Associates statement prominently below the masthead and repeat in the footer; review link-level disclosures near each promotion. Mark sponsored links appropriately. [Amazon disclosure help](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPXFHVYZMTGPUMPE); [Google outbound link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links).
5. Automate source collection, stale-content checks, link checks, drafts and event validation; require review for identity, claims, program policy, disclosure placement and publication. Automated drafts do not establish evidence, rankings or compliance.
