About

A shop that shows its homework

Crisp & Counter is an independent kitchen-gear publication built on a simple rule: if a claim can't be traced to an official source, it doesn't go on the page. The site is also an experiment in transparent affiliate publishing — the machinery is on display.

Editorial policy

  • Products lead, process follows. The front page is a shop; the verification machinery lives one level down in the Research desk.
  • Observed facts are dated. Prices, ratings and redirect targets carry the date we read them. They are photographs, not promises.
  • Blank beats guessed. Unconfirmed fields stay empty and are published on the Flagged for Review page with their source links.
  • No forecasts. We publish no conversion scores, revenue projections or commission estimates. The campaign ledger accepts only first-party reported figures.
  • Every commercial link is marked. rel="sponsored" on the link, a disclosure strip under the masthead, and the Amazon Associate statement in the footer of every page.
  • Working titles stay honest. Guide topics are editorial hypotheses, not claims about search volume or popularity.

Full affiliate disclosure

Crisp & Counter is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the FTC's guidance on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255) and the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement — see the source table below.

Method in brief

  1. Intake: each submitted link is recorded exactly as given, in the product-intent record, with intended fields kept strictly separate from observed fields.
  2. Observation: the link is fetched and its resolved Amazon listing read; titles, categories, ratings, prices and images are recorded with an observation date.
  3. Verification: each published field gets a row in the verification register with a source link to the official listing. Anything unconfirmed is left blank and flagged.
  4. Publication: pages are generated from that data, checked by automated gates (disclosure presence, sponsored marking, source coverage), then shipped.
