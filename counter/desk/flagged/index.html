FLG-001Intended ASIN fields are blank for all five submitted links
Amazon defines an ASIN as a 10-character code (S04). The submitted link tokens (e.g. B04G1MEwY) are short-link tokens, not confirmed ASINs, and nothing public binds a short code to a specific product identity. Per the record design, intended_asin / intended_listing_url / confirmation_date stay blank until the owner confirms them; observed redirect targets are stored separately and are never merged into the intent fields.
