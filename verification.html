Limits of this register

  • Retrieved pages are the first chunk of a long page. Prices and ratings visible in that chunk belong to related-product carousels, not necessarily to the landing ASIN, so no price or rating is attributed to any landing ASIN here.
  • A second request of LINK-01 returned a byte-identical response including the same Amazon session identifier, so it was a cached retrieval. It therefore does NOT establish whether the redirect target is stable or rotates. Recorded as inconclusive.
  • A single fetch per link cannot distinguish a per-request personalised fallback from a deterministic mapping. Confirm in a signed-in browser before publishing product identity.
  • link.amazon itself could not be inspected for domain ownership from this environment; treat ownership as unconfirmed (Q-02).
