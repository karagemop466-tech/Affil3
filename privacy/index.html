Privacy

Privacy, in one page

There is very little to disclose, because the page loads nothing from anyone else. This page describes exactly what happens when you visit.

Updated 2026-11-20No third-party requests
Current state, verified

This site makes no third-party requests. The stylesheet, the script and the icon are served from this domain. There are no fonts, no pixels, no social embeds and no advertising scripts on any page. No analytics collector is live yet.

What is loaded

One HTML file, one CSS file, one small JavaScript file and one SVG icon, all served from this site. Nothing is fetched from Amazon until you click a product link, and that click leaves this site.

What is recorded now

Nothing. The site includes code that emits five defined events to an endpoint, but no endpoint is configured, so no data leaves your browser. The measurement page describes what the events contain and what they deliberately exclude. When a collector is configured, it will be described here with its retention period before it is switched on.

What the events will contain, once enabled

  • The name of an event, such as a page view or a click on a product link.
  • The page path and the product identifier involved.
  • A coarse session identifier held in memory for the visit only, not stored on your device and not linked across visits.

They will not contain your name, email, IP address, device fingerprint, or any identifier that persists between visits.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. GitHub Pages, which hosts the files, may process technical request data as described in GitHub's own privacy statement; that is outside this site's control and worth knowing.

The email form

No email list is connected. Submitting the form currently records nothing and sends nothing. When a service is connected, the legal basis, the retention period and the unsubscribe route will be stated here before the form is switched on.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to opt out of. If that changes, an opt-out will exist before collection begins, not after.
