Campaign ledger

The ledger ships with its columns defined and no rows in it. Every number that goes in will come from a report you are authorised to see.

Why this page is empty

There is no authorised first-party report yet. An empty ledger with dated provenance columns is a better artefact than a plausible-looking estimate, because an empty one is obviously empty. The rules for filling it are below.
