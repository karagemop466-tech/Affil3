FLG-001 · None of the five submitted strings is an ASIN

highneeds reviewblocks launch: no
Finding
The brief supplies https://link.amazon/<token> for tokens B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj. Each token is 9 characters. Amazon ASINs are 10 characters. The strings behave as redirector tokens, not identifiers: four of them resolve to listings whose ASINs (B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DZFD6R9Z) share no characters with the submitted token.
Consequence
The 'intended ASIN' field is blank for all five rows. A link-level identity claim cannot be made. Anything published against a product uses the observed listing URL only.
You would do
Paste the ten-character ASINs (or full amazon.com/dp/ URLs) from Associates Central > Links > Special Links, or confirm that 'whatever the short link resolves to today' is the intended target.
Discovery step
In Associates Central, open each product, copy the ASIN from the URL bar, and drop the five ASINs into register/products.json under intended_asin. The build then stops showing the 'identity unconfirmed' state.
Withheld
no product-name or brand claim is attributed to the owner's intent; only observed listing titles are quoted as observations
Source
https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CDY277D3 · register row S06

FLG-002 · No prices, ratings, review counts, specs or commission rates are published anywhere

mediumneeds reviewblocks launch: no
Finding
Amazon's Program Policies are described (by two secondary sources) as permitting price and availability display only when the data is served by Amazon's own link/widget or pulled from the Creators/PA API, with a 24-hour refresh ceiling. This project holds no API credentials, and the policy text itself was not fetched from the official page (see S02).
Consequence
Comparison tables carry criteria, not numbers. The product cards carry a 'what we did not verify' panel instead of a spec sheet. This is the single biggest difference between this site and a typical affiliate site, and it is deliberate.
You would do
Either (a) keep the no-price stance and lean into 'we tell you how to judge it in 90 seconds' as the brand, or (b) obtain API credentials and let the builder render Amazon-supplied fields with a visible timestamp.
Discovery step
Check Associates Central for a 'Request API access' control and record the eligibility wording you see there; that simultaneously resolves S02, S07 and Q-09.
Withheld
all monetary and rating fields; scripts/checks.mjs fails the build on any currency pattern in shop/
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies · register row S02

FLG-003 · Compliance status of the supplied short links is unresolved - not claimed invalid

highneeds review - openblocks launch: no
Finding
Section 1 of the Operating Agreement requires links to 'properly use the special tagged link formats we provide'. The four resolved redirect chains ended at amazon.com/dp/ URLs whose recorded final URL carried no tag= parameter. That is an observation about what this environment saw, not proof of what an Associate-created short link transmits: Amazon's own onelink/Associates tooling can attach attribution at a step this fetch could not observe (redirect cookie, session-side tagging, or a later hop).
Consequence
The site publishes Special Links built in the documented tagged format with the site's tracking ID, and does not reuse the supplied short links as its primary outbound. The supplied links are not described anywhere on the site as invalid.
You would do
In Associates Central, generate one Special Links test link per product, click it in a clean browser, and confirm (i) the landing ASIN and (ii) that a session appears in Reports > Transactions. Test one product first, then scale.
Discovery step
One controlled test: new incognito window, click generated link, add any item to cart, do not buy, then read Reports for the session. Record the result in register/links.json test_log.
Withheld
the phrase 'invalid' is never used about the supplied links; the wording used is 'compliance status unresolved'
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement · register row S01

FLG-004 · P04 (token B0c8moW59) could not be resolved at all

mediumneeds reviewblocks launch: no
Finding
Six attempts (2026-11-20, two windows) to resolve https://link.amazon/B0c8moW59: three landed on an Amazon anti-bot interstitial (amazon.com/checkpoint/continue) and three failed at transport. No listing was reached, so no ASIN, title, brand or category is known. A sibling session had earlier mis-recorded a checkpoint as a dead link and corrected it, so this row was deliberately retried before recording the negative - the outcome held.
Consequence
P04 has no product page on the site and appears in the register as UNRESOLVED (6 attempts logged in products.json). Nothing about it is guessed - not its category, not its brand, not even whether it is a kitchen product.
You would do
Open the link once in your own browser and paste the resulting amazon.com/dp/ URL, or replace the link.
Discovery step
Resolve manually in a normal browser session (which will not trip the checkpoint), copy the final URL, then re-run npm run build.
Withheld
the product page for P04 and its navigation entry

FLG-005 · No site URL is registered to the tracking ID, and the site has no analytics deployed

highneeds reviewblocks launch: no
Finding
Associates Central requires each Site where Program Content appears to be listed and approved, with the tracking ID attached to it. The Pages URL (https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/Affil3/) is a preview-grade subpath of a github.io domain; it has not been registered and cannot be registered from this environment. The click counter in shopnow/assets/track.js posts to an endpoint that is implemented locally (scripts/collect.mjs) but not deployed.
Consequence
Traffic measurement is currently a stub: events buffer in the reader’s own tab (sessionStorage, cleared when the tab closes) and are transmitted only if a collector endpoint is configured, and monetisation should not be attempted on a github.io subpath.
You would do
Register a domain you control, point Pages at it, list the exact URL in Associates Central > Account Settings > Site Settings, and create tracking ID shopnow047c-20 if it does not already exist.
Discovery step
Check whether shopnow047c-20 already appears in Account Settings > Tracking IDs. If it does not, create it before publishing any live link.
Withheld
the endpoint URL is left as a documented placeholder rather than pointed at a third-party collector
Source
https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home/accounturldetails · register row S09

FLG-006 · Content is AI-assisted and has had no human editorial sign-off

highneeds reviewblocks launch: no
Finding
Every guide on this site was drafted in an automated session. No named human editor reviewed it, and no hands-on testing of any product was performed by this project. The site says so on the methodology page and on each guide.
Consequence
The guides are written as *what to look for and what to ask*, never as test results. Words like 'we tested', 'our lab', 'we compared side by side' do not appear, and checks.mjs rejects them.
You would do
Either adopt the byline as-is (a desk, not a person) or assign a human editor before the site is used for monetised traffic. Do not add a person's name or credentials to 'about' without their agreement.
Discovery step
Answer Q-12 (who signs off) and Q-13 (is a named byline available). Until then the site keeps the disclosure line.
Withheld
no fabricated author biography, no fabricated test data, no invented ratings

FLG-007 · Repo state did not match the brief's implied history

mediumneeds review - informationalblocks launch: no
Finding
At base commit 847ae37 the repository contained one file, README.md (8 bytes). The brief refers to an existing 'careful source register', rows SG-27..SG-30, flags FLG-003, and sections S01/S07/S24. No such artifacts exist in any commit on any branch of this repository. Nine sibling session branches exist, each containing only README.md + COORDINATION.md.
Consequence
The referenced registers were re-established forward on this branch with the identifiers the brief used, and labelled as newly created rather than recovered. If the real prior work exists in another repository or branch, this site's registers must be reconciled against it before trusting continuity.
You would do
Point this session at the repository/branch that holds the earlier register if one exists, so the two can be diffed row by row.
Discovery step
git log --all --oneline --name-only on the correct remote; diff the prior register against register/.
Withheld
the site does not claim continuity with an earlier audit; it presents the register it can stand behind

FLG-010 · No traffic, ranking, revenue or conversion forecast is made anywhere

informationalclosed by designblocks launch: no
Finding
Section 4 of the Operating Agreement records that Amazon makes no representation about traffic or commission income an Associate can expect. Nothing in this repository measures search demand, and the campaign ledger is empty by design. A revenue model built on those two facts would be invention.
Consequence
The site publishes no 'expected earnings', no 'X% conversion rate', no 'this keyword has N volume'. The ledger exists with typed columns and zero rows, and the methodology page explains why.
You would do
After 2-4 weeks of first-party data, decide whether to fund an SEO tool account; only then does volume-based topic selection become defensible.
Discovery step
Search Console query export (owner action Q-08) is the cheapest first-party substitute for keyword volume and is enough to start.
Withheld
every forecast
