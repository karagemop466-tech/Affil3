## Read this first

Everything on this site is written for one moment: you, standing at a listing page, trying to work out whether this machine is right for your kitchen. Four guides cover the four decisions that moment contains - **shape**, **capacity**, **cleanup and counter cost**, and **whether a multi-function claim is real**. A fifth teaches you to read the listing itself in ninety seconds.

## The two questions that decide almost everything

1. **Basket or cabinet?** A shallow round basket you shake, or a door you open like an oven. Start [here](/guides/basket-vs-oven-style/).
2. **How many people are you really cooking for, and will you wash it?** Start with [capacity](/guides/capacity-checklist/) and [the counter test](/guides/cleanup-and-counter-space/).

## What we publish, and what we refuse to

We publish: the products, the exact official listing for each, the date we confirmed it, and the specific questions we think are worth your time.

We do not publish: prices, availability, star ratings, review counts, or any test we did not run. Amazon's terms let a site display price and availability only when Amazon supplies the data through its own link or its catalog API, and this project holds no API credentials - so rather than post a number that quietly rots, we post the question the number answers. Every withheld field is named, in the open, on [Flagged for review](/flagged-for-review/).
