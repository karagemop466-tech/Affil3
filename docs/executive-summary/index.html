Executive summary — ShopNow 047

Prepared: 2026-11-20 · Repository: karagemop466-tech/Affil3 · Branch merged: arena/59aebafb-affil3 → main Tracking ID: shopnow047c-20 · Marketplace: Amazon.com (US)

1. What was asked, in one paragraph

Build an automated Amazon-associates publishing platform: product research, trending and high-converting product identification, price/review/competition/commission analysis, SEO-optimised articles, comparisons and buying guides, published and tracked affiliate links, campaign optimisation — with automation and scale first-class and Associates compliance treated as a gate rather than an afterthought. On top of that, turn the register into a shop a buyer can act on, make it findable, add measurement before optimisation, and provide a reviewable suggestion board plus a list of the questions that must be answered before a decision is defensible.

4. Decisions taken, and why

  1. No prices, ratings, review counts or availability anywhere on the site. The permission to

display them is gated behind API credentials this project does not have, and the policy wording could not be read in full. So the strict reading wins and a build check rejects any currency figure reaching the output. Cost: less "shop feel". Benefit: no stale claim can exist.

  1. Short links are not called invalid. Observed final URLs carried no tag= parameter — an

observation about what this environment saw, not proof about attribution. The site therefore publishes links generated in the documented /dp/<ASIN>?tag= form and leaves FLG-003 open.

  1. P04 gets no product page. A card would require inventing a brand, a category or an image.

It appears in the register as UNRESOLVED and nowhere else.

  1. No hands-on testing language, ever. Nothing was tested. Guides are written as *what to

check and how to read it*, and each one states the absence of testing inside the article.

  1. Original imagery only. Amazon catalog images are Program Content; the licensed path is the

API or an Amazon-served widget. Three generated editorial illustrations are captioned as illustration, so the site never implies product photography it did not license.

  1. Brand derived, and labelled as derived. "ShopNow 047" comes from the tracking ID because

the repo had no brand asset. It is one string in register/site.json.

  1. Campaign ledger ships empty. The brief's own instruction: populate only from authorised

first-party reports. An "industry average" seed was explicitly rejected (SG-26).

5. What was built

Site — 23 published pages generated from the register: publication front page; 6 question-shaped guides; product pages for the 4 resolved listings; 4 decision-shape clusters; a 7-row shape comparison; methodology, about, disclosure, privacy; and the machinery set (dashboard, source register, suggestions, open questions, executive summary, flagged-for-review); client-side search with a static index; RSS feed, sitemap, robots, humans.txt; JSON-LD (WebSite, Article, Product, CollectionPage, ItemList, BreadcrumbList, Dataset) on every page.

Machinery — scripts/build.mjs (zero dependencies; every page, feed and sitemap entry generated), scripts/checks.mjs (11 automated gates, exit-1 on failure), scripts/collect.mjs (self-hosted NDJSON event collector), docs/assets/track.js (5 events, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no third party), GitHub Actions CI that builds, checks, and proves docs/ is not stale.

Data — 11 source rows, 5 product-intent records, 22 suggestion rows, 22 open questions, 10 flags, 12 compliance controls, 5 link candidates with a one-link test protocol, and a typed ingest schema (register/schema/research_snapshot.schema.json) that refuses a 9-character ASIN — the exact failure mode of the supplied links.

Measurement — five events defined with fields, privacy notes and conversion roles; collector contract specified; ledger schema with integrity rules and read-time floors (a ratio is not shown until its denominator clears 50–100).

6. Where the model stops short of the ambition

The brief describes a self-optimising revenue engine. What can honestly exist today is the publishing and compliance half, plus the interfaces for the research and optimisation half. The gap is not effort, it is credentials:

  • No catalog API → no automated product research, price analysis, review mining, or

commission modelling. The eligibility gate sits behind the sales the data would enable. This is the central constraint and it is reported rather than routed around.

  • No registered site → no attribution, therefore no clicks or conversions to count.
  • No traffic yet → therefore no query data, therefore topic selection is a stated hypothesis,

not a measured decision. Search Console verification is the cheapest way to change that (SG-29), and it is free.

Anyone who claims this site will earn a given amount is guessing; Amazon's own s.4 says no representation about traffic or income is made. No forecast appears anywhere here.
