Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

"Multi-function" sounds like one appliance doing the job of five. Sometimes it's true; sometimes it's a lot of features you'll never use. Ask these questions before you buy.

The ten questions

  1. What will I cook most often? List the top five meals. If the appliance can't handle them well, the extra functions don't matter.
  2. For how many people? Capacity needs follow the household, not the ad copy.
  3. Where will it live, and for how long? Measure the counter, and decide whether it stays out or goes in a cabinet.
  4. Which functions will I actually use weekly? Be honest: a "10-in-1" appliance is only as good as the three functions you reach for.
  5. How is cleanup? Which parts are dishwasher-safe, and how many parts are there?
  6. What have verified buyers said? Check ratings and review counts on the official listing — and read recent reviews, not just the headline number.
  7. Is the price consistent with the features I'll use? Compare the listed price against the functions you answered in question 4.
  8. Does it have safety features I need? Auto shut-off, cool-touch exteriors, and stable bases matter in a busy kitchen.
  9. What's the return window? Confirm it before you need it — especially for a big-ticket multi-function purchase.
  10. Am I buying because I need it, or because it's trending? Trends fade; your cooking routine doesn't.
