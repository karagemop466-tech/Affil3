About

Executive summary

What this is. The Countertop Edit is a source-first Amazon affiliate marketing site built around five submitted product links (tracking ID shopnow047c-20). It leads with the products as a shop front, keeps every verification surface reachable from a single navigation group and the footer, and carries the machinery — measurement, compliance gates, and a suggestions/decision workflow — behind the scenes.

Four resolved to confirmed Amazon listings and were read line by line against the official listing pages: a CHEFMAN 6-quart dual basket air fryer oven, an Emeril Lagasse 26QT French-door air fryer toaster oven, a Chefman 8-quart TurboFry, and a CHEF iQ Sense smart wireless meat thermometer (Gen3). What the site guarantees. Every table row links to its official source. Every outbound product link is marked as sponsored and tagged rel="sponsored". The affiliate disclosure sits above the fold on every page and repeats in the footer. Intent and observed redirect are recorded in separate fields that are never merged. The campaign ledger is empty and well-defined — entries will only come from authorized first-party reports, and no conversion score or revenue forecast is presented.

What is pending. Methodology

How the work was done

  1. Source register first. Every claim was checked against an official source — Amazon listing pages, the Associates Operating Agreement and Program Policies, Amazon's selling glossary, Google Search Central docs, FTC guidance, and GitHub Pages docs.
  2. Line-by-line verification. Products were resolved live; titles, brands, categories, prices, ratings, review counts, and specs were read from the official listing pages and recorded with the date of reading.
  3. No inference. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, it was left blank and flagged (see Flagged for Review). Nothing was filled in by assumption.
  4. Automate what can be automated, gate what cannot. Link checking, sitemap building, and intake validation are scripted; Special Link compliance and product-intent confirmation are human gates in the workflow.
  5. Lead with the products. The front page is a shop front; the verification surfaces live in a single navigation group and the footer.
