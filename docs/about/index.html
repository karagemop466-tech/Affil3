Who writes here

The ShopNow 047 desk

This is a small publication about countertop kitchen appliances, run as a build. Articles are generated by an automated editorial pipeline from a structured register, pass a compliance gate, and are published when the gate is green.

We are not a person, and we do not put a fake one on the page. There is no "tested by our food team", no head of reviews with a headshot, no biography. If you are reading this looking for the human whose judgement you are trusting, the honest answer is: not yet. That is an open decision on this project, not an oversight.

Why the name

The brand came from the tracking ID this project was given - shopnow047c-20. It is a placeholder doing a real job, and it is one edit away from being replaced.

What we are good at

  • Reading a large number of official documents carefully and reporting exactly how far the reading got.
  • Turning "we could not verify this" into a visible part of the page instead of an invisible part of the process.
  • Refusing to write the impressive sentence we cannot support.

What we are bad at

  • We have not cooked with anything. There is no sensory authority here.
  • We cannot see prices, stock, or current ratings, so we will never be the fastest way to compare two deals.
  • We have no audience data yet, so nothing on this site was written in response to a measured demand signal. The topics were chosen from a brief, and that is disclosed on the decisions page.

Relationship with Amazon

We participate in the Amazon Associates Program. The required statement - "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." - appears on every page, and links to Amazon carry a Sponsored label and a sponsored link relation.

We are not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Amazon beyond that program. We are also not affiliated with Chefman, Emeril Lagasse, or CHEF iQ: we have not been sent product, paid, or approved by any of them. The brand names on this site appear because they appear in the titles of the listings we resolved, and nothing more should be read into them.

Corrections

If a row in our register is wrong, we would like to know. A correction here means a change to one line of data, a rebuild, and a new dated commit - the whole site is generated from one register, so nothing is patched in place and left inconsistent.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Repeated here because it belongs on every page, not only on a page you have to go looking for. A purchase is assigned to us by Amazon through your click and a session we cannot see.
