About

How the Countertop Journal is researched, written and checked, and what the site will and will not claim.

What this is

Countertop Journal is a set of buyer guides for kitchen countertop appliances. Each guide asks the questions a buyer should answer first. Product cards link to Amazon listings for the five submitted products.

How we check a product

  1. We open the submitted link and follow its redirects.
  2. We read the official listing page: the title, the category breadcrumb and the ASIN in the address.
  3. We record the destination in a separate field from the submitted link.
  4. If a value cannot be read on an official page, it is left blank and flagged. Nothing is filled in from memory or from other sites.

The steps and results are in the product register and the source register.

What we do not show

  • Prices and star ratings, as Amazon's policies require (SRC-02).
  • Commission figures, until they can be read from Amazon's schedule (FLG-010).
  • Campaign results, until there are first-party reports (Ledger).

Who writes this

Guides are published under the Editorial team byline. A named author with a bio is an open decision (SUG-05).

How we keep pages current

Every product row and guide carries a "checked" or "updated" date. Corrections and open items are listed on the Flagged for Review page, with source links.
