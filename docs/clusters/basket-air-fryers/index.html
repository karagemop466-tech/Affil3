A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity — Quarts are a volume, and a volume is not a dinner. Eight checks that turn a number in a title into a decision you can defend at the counter.

The checks this shape makes harder

  • Floor area, not volume. A round basket's usable capacity is its single-layer footprint; the quart figure includes headspace nobody can fill.
  • Shake access. If the basket has to come fully out to turn the food, that is a workflow you are agreeing to twice a night.
  • Dishwasher geometry. Basket height versus rack height is a real, checkable incompatibility.
