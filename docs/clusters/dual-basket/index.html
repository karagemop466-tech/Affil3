The checks this shape makes harder

  • Two small cavities, not one big one. Anything taller than one basket's interior simply does not fit, however the total is advertised.
  • Sync finish is the feature. If you rarely cook two foods at once, you are paying for a mode you will not use.
  • One outlet, one draw. Both zones running together is the peak load; the manual, not the title, states it.
