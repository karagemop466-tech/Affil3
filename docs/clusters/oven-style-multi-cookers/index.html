Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance — Eight functions in a product title can be eight real modes or one fan with marketing attached. A short interrogation you can run at the listing.

The checks this shape makes harder

  • Door arc and counter depth. A downward door needs front clearance no spec table will complain about.
  • Which functions are load-bearing. A low temperature floor is what separates real dehydration from a warm fan.
  • More parts, more tax. Door glass and crumb trays are where owners stop, and where resentment starts.
