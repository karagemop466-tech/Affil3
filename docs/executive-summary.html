Review · executive summary

Executive summary

Checked 2026-11-20. Every figure links to a source on the source register.

What was done

  • Five submitted short links were followed and the destination recorded for each (see product records).
  • Each intended identifier was checked against the official ASIN format (SRC-12).
  • The Associates Operating Agreement and Program Policies were read for the statement, link format, and price-display rules (SRC-01, SRC-02).
  • The site was built as a static GitHub Pages site with a front page, category pages, guides, dashboard, and a review section.

What is verified

  • Three observed destinations are Amazon product listings with recorded titles, ASINs and breadcrumbs (P1, P2, P5). P3 and P4 are open; see FLG-02.
  • The required Associates statement and the FTC disclosure placement are implemented on every page.
  • Every Amazon product link carries rel="sponsored".

What is not verified, and why it matters

  • 0 of 5 intended identifiers pass the 10-character ASIN format. All five are 9 characters with lowercase letters. (FLG-01)
  • 3 of 5 confirmed links reach a different ASIN than the one in the link (P1, P2, P5). P3 and P4 are open: this session's re-fetch on 2026-11-20 matched its earlier reading, but another session's board records the two short links the other way round, so the mapping is not settled. (FLG-02, FLG-03)
  • 0 of 4 observed destinations contain the tracking ID. (FLG-04)
  • No prices, ratings, or commission rates are verified, so pricing and commission potential cannot be analysed yet. (FLG-09, FLG-10)
  • The click counter and analytics endpoint are built but gated, because GitHub Pages cannot store events. (FLG-11)

Decisions needed before scaling

  1. Confirm each intended identifier from its original listing (Q01–Q02).
  2. Confirm the tracking ID and Site registration in Associates Central (Q03, Q05).
  3. Decide the link approach: test one Special Link first, or hold (S02).
  4. Choose a measurement path and define the five events (S04, Q06).

Not done in this build

  • No Associates test link was generated. That needs account access.
  • No Search Console verification. That needs the owner's Google account.
  • No self-hosted analytics endpoint. That needs a server.
