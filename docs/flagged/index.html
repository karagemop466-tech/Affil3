FLG-001

Submitted tokens are 9 characters — not ASIN format

All five submitted codes (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj) are 9 characters. Amazon defines an ASIN as a unique Amazon-assigned number for a product; guidance consistently describes the ASIN as a 10-character alphanumeric identifier. The codes are short-link tokens, not ASINs.

This does not claim the links are invalid. It records the token-vs-ASIN distinction only.

Sources: Amazon A-to-Z glossary
