About the publication · our editorial promise

A useful kitchen guide should show its work.

Countertop Notes is a small editorial shop for practical kitchen decisions: what fits, what you will use, and which details need a better source before they can guide a purchase.

Authorship

Written by the editorial desk

This first edition uses “Countertop Notes Editorial Desk” as a transparent desk byline. No individual author, hands-on testing, product ownership, or firsthand results were supplied, so we do not claim any.

How we compare

Questions before rankings

Our current guides are decision frameworks, not product tests. We do not publish a winner, price comparison, review summary, or product claim without confirming the exact item and attaching an appropriate source.

Sources

Primary pages over guesses

We use official Amazon sources for Associates policy and observed listings, FTC guidance for endorsement disclosures, Google Search Central for discoverability, and GitHub documentation for Pages deployment.
