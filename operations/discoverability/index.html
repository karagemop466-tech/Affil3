Discoverability

One site to index, and a reason to rank it

A search engine needs a canonical version of every page, a map of them, and a reason to prefer them to the alternatives. Here is what this build provides for each.

What is missing

Verification and submission. The property could not be verified from this environment, so the sitemap has not been submitted and no query data exists. This is the single most important open item for organic traffic. FLG-007

Search demand evidence. The six guides were chosen on editorial judgement. Nothing on this site claims a search volume, a difficulty score or a trend, because no keyword tool was consulted. Q-05

Links from anywhere else. Nothing points at this site yet, and no link-building tactic is proposed here, because the honest ones take time and the dishonest ones are a penalty risk.

A publishing cadence that compounds

  1. Ship two verified guides a month. Each one adds a sitemap entry and a feed item automatically, with no manual bookkeeping.
  2. Read queries after four to six weeks. Write the next guide in the language people actually used, not the language we guessed.
  3. Update rather than pile up. A revised guide with a new dateModified is worth more than two thin ones.
  4. Retire what stops being true. When a product listing changes materially, the page that described it is corrected or removed, not left to rot.
Rule of thumb

One verified page that answers one question beats ten pages that summarise a category. The sitemap does not care how many URLs it holds; a crawler only keeps the ones worth keeping.
