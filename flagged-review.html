Open questions before a product decision

  1. What exact product and category does each submitted link represent? The product category itself is unverified; air-fryer guide topics are editorial hypotheses, not identification. Confirm by inspecting the destination in an authorized workflow and finding the product’s official detail page. Save the intended product identifier separately from the destination observed during a test.
  2. What are the intended ASINs and official Amazon listing URLs? Confirm each on Amazon’s product detail page or authorized Associates tools; record the source and date. Amazon describes ASINs as 10-character alphanumeric identifiers.
  3. Are the supplied links currently usable as compliant Special Links? Their status is unresolved. Test one link per product inside the enrolled Associates workflow; verify the actual destination and that the tracking report attributes it to the selected Tracking ID. Do not infer invalidity from the abbreviated path.
  4. Is shopnow047c-20 active and enrolled for the actual website? Confirm account status, site approval and marketplace in Associates Central. The tracking ID here is supplied by the project owner; no account access or first-party verification was available.
  5. Which product facts can be stated? Confirm product name, model, dimensions, capacity, price, availability, ratings, review count, features, warranty and images individually against eligible official sources. Until each is confirmed, keep it blank; prices and availability are time-sensitive.
  6. What does the audience actually search for? Connect Search Console after the site is live, wait for actual query data and evaluate impressions/clicks by page. No search-volume or trend claims are made now.
  7. What is the measurement endpoint and privacy notice? GitHub Pages cannot execute a server-side API. Choose and deploy a first-party backend, define retention/access controls and consent/privacy text before describing analytics as active.
  8. Where will conversion/order facts come from? Use authorized first-party Associates reports only. No orders, commissions, rates, forecast or conversion score are in evidence.
  9. Who owns editorial, compliance and updates? Name accountable reviewers, define a publish/update date, and schedule policy and product-link checks before scaling.
