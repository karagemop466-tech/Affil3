A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Working title — editorial hypothesis. Not a claim about popularity, search volume, or product superiority, and not a recommendation of any specific product.

Capacity is the most-overstated number in air-fryer shopping. This checklist is a working tool for matching capacity to how you actually cook — not a list of products to buy.

Capacity checklist

  • Household size: 1–2 people often suit smaller baskets; 3–4 or more usually need larger capacity — but cooking style matters more than headcount alone.
  • Typical batch size: Are you cooking one portion at a time, or full family meals in a single cycle?
  • Food types: Fries and wings need less vertical room than whole chickens, roasts, or layered baking.
  • Counter space: Measure the spot before comparing dimensions; include clearance for opening the basket or door.
  • Storage: Where does it go between uses? Heavier, larger units are harder to stow in a cabinet.
  • Listed vs. usable capacity: Check whether a quoted volume refers to total basket volume or usable cooking space.

What this checklist does not do

It does not rank products, declare a "best" capacity, or imply that any submitted product on this site meets any of these criteria. Product submissions are unverified — see Products →.
