A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity is one of the first numbers you see and one of the easiest to misread.

By Affil3 editorial team · Published November 20, 2026 · Last reviewed November 20, 2026 · Editorial policy

The short version

  • Capacity is quoted differently on different listings: total, per basket, or usable cooking area.
  • Compare the number you will actually use, then check the manual for the maximum fill line.
  • A bigger basket needs more counter space and more clearance under the cabinets.

On this page

  1. 1. Check whether the number is total or per basket
  2. 2. Measure the food you actually cook
  3. 3. Compare time and temperature, not only size
  4. 4. Test a load before you rely on it
  5. 5. Write down what you could not confirm

Working title. This checklist does not claim that any capacity is better or that any size will cook faster.

1. Check whether the number is total or per basket

Some listings give a total for the unit and a separate figure for each basket. The observed title for one dual-basket Chefman listing gives '6 Quart' for the unit and '2 Independent 3Qt' baskets. Those numbers measure different things, so write down which one you are comparing.

2. Measure the food you actually cook

  • Measure your largest regular item and the number of pieces you cook at once.
  • Use the inside dimensions of the basket or rack from the manual. Outside dimensions include the handle and the housing.
  • Note whether the manual gives a recommended maximum load.

3. Compare time and temperature, not only size

A larger basket is not automatically faster. For the same food, compare the time and temperature charts in each manual. If a manual has no chart for your food, record that as a gap.

4. Test a load before you rely on it

If the manual gives no maximum load, cook in batches first and note how evenly the food browns. Keep your notes with the date and the food, so you can repeat the test on another unit.

5. Write down what you could not confirm

If the listing does not state a capacity in both total and per-basket terms, mark the row as unconfirmed. Do not fill the gap from a different listing.

What this guide does not claim

  • No capacity figure on this site has been verified beyond the wording of the listing title.
  • We do not publish measured basket dimensions, because we have not measured them.
  • Cooking results depend on food shape and the recipe, not on litres alone.

Questions buyers ask next

Is a bigger air fryer always better?

No. A larger basket takes more counter space, can be harder to clean, and heats up more slowly when it is nearly empty. Match the size to what you cook most weeks.

Why does the listing say 8 Qt while the drawer looks smaller?

Listings often quote total capacity, and some quote capacity across two baskets. Read the manual for the usable fill level before you rely on the number.
