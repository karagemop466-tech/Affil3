Air fryer or toaster oven: which one actually replaces the other?

One appliance or two? Start with what you already cook, then with what you can reach.

By Affil3 editorial team · Published November 20, 2026 · Last reviewed November 20, 2026 · Editorial policy

The short version

  • Basket units are for shaking and tossing; oven-style units are for trays, racks and flat food.
  • An oven-style air fryer needs clearance in front for the door, not just width on the counter.
  • If you cannot decide, wait a week and note which shape you missed.

Editorial guide. It compares two appliance shapes, not two products. We have not tested the products listed on this site.

The shapes, in plain terms

A basket air fryer is a drawer with a fan above it. You pull the drawer out, shake or turn the food, and push it back. An oven-style air fryer is a small countertop oven with a door, racks or a tray, and often several functions beyond air frying.

The difference is not just size. It is how you reach the food and how much of the counter the thing occupies when the door is open.

What an oven-style unit buys you

  • Racks and trays that take flat or long food: a small pizza, toast, a tray of vegetables.
  • Several functions in one box, which can replace a toaster as well.
  • A door you can open and reach into, instead of a drawer you shake.

What it costs you

  • Depth. A door that opens forward needs clearance in front, and the unit is usually deeper than a drawer.
  • Preheat time. A larger cavity generally takes longer to come up to temperature, as the manual will state.
  • One more surface to clean: the door glass, the racks, the crumb tray.

A decision rule that works

  1. List the five meals you cook most. If they are all one-pan or one-tray, an oven-style unit fits.
  2. If they need shaking and tossing, a basket fits better.
  3. If you want toast and air frying from one box, count how much counter space you have with the door open.
  4. If you cannot decide, buy neither yet: cook for a week and note which one you missed.

What this guide does not claim

  • We have not tested either shape side by side.
  • This is a decision checklist, not a product ranking.

Questions buyers ask next

Can an oven-style air fryer replace a toaster?

Its listing and manual may name a toast function. Whether it replaces your toaster depends on the slot or rack size and how you like your toast, so read the manual rather than the title.

Does an air fryer replace a microwave?

Generally no. They heat food in different ways and at different speeds. Nothing on this site supports a claim that one replaces the other.
