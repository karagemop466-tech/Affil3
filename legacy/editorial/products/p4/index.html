Suits you if

A quick spot check at the grill, not a leave-in monitor.

Skip it if

You want continuous monitoring through a long cook. That is the CHEF iQ Sense on this site, not this.

Questions this answers

  • How fast is a reading?
  • Can I wash it under the tap?
  • Will it stay in the drawer or on the fridge?

What you gain

  • One-second read, so the oven door is open for less time.
  • NIST-certified +/-0.5 F accuracy, stated by the manufacturer.
  • Folds shut and switches itself off, which is why people keep using it.
  • Built-in magnet.

What it costs you

  • This is a spot-check tool. No leave-in rating is published, so it is not a substitute for a probe you leave in the meat.
  • No IP rating is stated on the manufacturer page, only 'waterproof design'.
  • Battery type and replacement interval are not stated on the page.
