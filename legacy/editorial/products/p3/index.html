Suits you if

One big batch, one basket, no dividers to work around.

Skip it if

What you gain

  • Eight quarts in one basket — no divider limiting a long item.
  • Four presets and two dials' worth of decisions; low learning curve.
  • Top-rack dishwasher-safe basket.
  • 400 F ceiling is enough for most frozen and fresh-cook jobs.

What it costs you
