Observed redirect targets are kept in a separate field and are never merged with the intended listing fields.

Source standards: Amazon Associates Program policies (Amazon Associates help) and FTC disclosure guidance (FTC endorsement guides) are linked for manual review. Neither could be cross-checked against live Amazon data in this environment — see flagged items.
