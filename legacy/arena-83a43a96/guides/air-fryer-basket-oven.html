Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Working title — editorial hypothesis. This is not a claim about popularity, search volume, or product superiority, and not a recommendation of any specific product.

Buyers shopping for an air fryer usually start with a form-factor question: basket-style or oven-style. This guide is a working framework for what to compare before looking at any listing — not a shortlist of products.

Questions this guide addresses

  • How much counter space is available, and where will the appliance live when it is not in use?
  • How many people are you typically cooking for?
  • Do you mostly cook single-layer items (fries, nuggets) or multi-rack meals (baking, roasting, dehydrating)?
  • How important is visibility into the cooking basket during use?
  • How easy does cleanup need to be: removable non-stick basket, dishwasher-safe parts, or manual wiping?

What to compare

Form-factor considerations typically include basket capacity (quarts/litres), footprint and height, rack layers, temperature range, timer type, control interface, removable-part dishwasher safety, and warranty length. None of these factors, by themselves, determine which product is right for a particular household.
