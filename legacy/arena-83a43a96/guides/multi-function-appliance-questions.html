Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Working title — editorial hypothesis. Not a claim about popularity, search volume, or product superiority, and not a recommendation of any specific product.

"Multi-function" sounds like value, but every extra function adds cost, complexity, and something to clean. This question set helps you decide whether you will actually use the functions you are paying for.

Pre-purchase questions

  • Which functions will I use at least once a week?
  • Which functions am I paying for but could live without?
  • Does adding functions reduce performance at the core task (e.g., air frying in a multi-function oven)?
  • How much counter and storage space does the appliance need?
  • How many parts must be cleaned after each function is used?
  • What is the warranty, and who honors it?
  • What is the total cost, including accessories needed for each function?
