Suits you if

Two different foods, two different timings, one compact footprint.

Skip it if

You regularly cook one large item, or you want a single wide basket for a big batch.

Questions this answers

  • Will two foods actually finish together?
  • Can I see the food without pulling the basket?
  • Does it fit under a cabinet?

What you gain

  • A window on each basket means fewer heat-losing peeks.
  • Sync Finish lands two foods at the same moment.
  • Two 3-quart zones are easier to clean than one wide chamber.
  • 1700 W and a 450 F ceiling give a real crisping finish.

What it costs you

  • Six quarts is the sum of two small baskets, not one big cooking space.
  • No preset count published by the manufacturer, so don't shop it as a preset machine.
  • 13.3 lb and 13.38 in deep, so measure the counter and the cabinet clearance.
