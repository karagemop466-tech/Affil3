Disclosure and method

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

This page exists so that the disclosure is not only in a footer. It is repeated under the masthead on every page, it appears in the footer on every page, and it is on this page twice.

What we do not display, and why

No prices. Amazon's Program Policies allow a site to show prices and availability only if Amazon serves the link that displays them, or if the data comes from the Creators API or the Product Advertising API. This site does neither, so it shows no price at all. Every button says “check current price at Amazon” and sends you to the source.

No star ratings or review counts. The same policies restrict displaying customer reviews and star ratings to those obtained through the Product Advertising API. None appear here.

No price tracking or alerts. The policies prohibit price tracking and price alerting functionality. This site stores no price at all.

What we do display

Specifications published by the manufacturer, each with a source ID and the date it was read. Where two official pages disagree, the field is left blank and the conflict is published on the Flagged for review page.

Tracked outbound links

Every outbound product link must carry tag=shopnow047c-20 as a URL parameter. The build refuses to render a button that lacks it. Right now that means no outbound button renders, because the supplied links carry no tag. That is described on FLG-003 rather than worked around.

Measurement

Five events, no cookies, no identifiers, no fingerprinting: page views, outbound product clicks, guide reads, suggestion votes, and disclosure views. The endpoint is unset in this build, so nothing is recorded. When it is set, the privacy policy must name it before traffic arrives.

Editorial position

This site compares published specifications. It does not test products and never claims to. Where a recommendation depends on hands-on experience, the site says so instead of implying experience it does not have — which is also what the FTC's Endorsement Guides require of an endorser.
