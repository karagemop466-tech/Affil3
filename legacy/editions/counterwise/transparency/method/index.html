The machinery

How this site is built, and what refuses to build

Three commands stand between a data edit and a published page: python3 tools/build.py, then python3 tools/gate.py, then a commit. The generator has no opinions; the gate has seventeen of them.

The pipeline

  1. data/*.json and content/guides/*.md are the only place facts are written.
  2. tools/build.py renders every page, the sitemap, the feed and this report from those files. Nothing is hand-written in docs/.
  3. tools/gate.py checks the finished HTML against the register: sources, disclosure placement, tagged and marked outbound links, resolvable internal links, banned voice, structured data that never overclaims, and output freshness by content hash.
  4. A failed gate exits non-zero, so the CI workflow on every push fails and the site cannot be published broken or unsourced.

Where images come from

Drawn for this site and captioned as illustration. No Amazon product imagery appears anywhere, because program imagery is licensed through program tooling rather than scraped from a page, and because a manufacturer's render is the visual signature of a template site. That reverses when an authorized feed exists: Q-05.
