Campaign ledger

An empty ledger with strict rules

This starts with zero rows because no authorised first-party report has been received yet. The schema is fixed now so that no future number can be entered without a source and a date.

0 rowsProvenance requiredUpdated 2026-11-20
For the reviewer

A ledger with invented rows is worse than no ledger. Nothing below is a forecast, a benchmark or an industry average. When a row is added it must carry the report it came from and the date it was pulled.

Rules for adding a row

  1. Source must be an authorised first-party report. The Amazon Associates dashboard, the Creators API, or a self-hosted analytics collector. Not an estimate, not a third-party blog, not a scrape.
  2. Timestamp every row. A number without a date is a rumour.
  3. Never mix a click count with an order count. They come from different systems with different attribution windows, and the session rules matter.
  4. Leave a field empty rather than estimating it. An empty cell is honest; a filled guess is not.
  5. No revenue forecast. This ledger records what happened, never what might.

What the attribution window means for this ledger

Amazon's Commission Income Statement defines a session as beginning when a customer clicks a Special Link and ending at the first of: 24 hours elapsing, the customer placing an order, or the customer clicking a different Associates link. Cart purchases can still qualify up to 89 days after the initial click. So a click today and an order next week can belong to the same row, and a click today and an order after someone clicked a competitor's link cannot. C-18

dateISO dateThe date the report covers, not the date it was read.
sourcestringThe report the number came from. Must be an authorised first-party system: Associates dashboard, Creators API, or the self-hosted collector. No third-party estimates.
channelstringWhere the traffic came from: organic search, direct, referral, email. Must be reported by the system, not inferred.
landing_pagepathThe page the session started on. Use the path, not a tracking URL.
eligible_sessionsintegerOnly sessions that could have produced a qualifying purchase. Leave blank if the report does not distinguish them.
outbound_clicksintegerClicks recorded by our own collector. Do not substitute Amazon's click report here; it measures something else.
reported_ordersintegerOrdered items as reported by Amazon, with the report date. Not a conversion rate until both sides are measured over the same window.
reported_earningsdecimalCommission income as reported, in USD, with the month it was earned. Payments arrive about 60 days later; record the earning month, not the payment date.
provenancestringWho pulled the number, from which system, and when. A row without this field is invalid and must be deleted.

Payment timing, for planning only

Commission income is paid approximately 60 days after the end of the calendar month in which it was earned, per the Commission Income Statement. That is a payment schedule, not a revenue projection. C-25
