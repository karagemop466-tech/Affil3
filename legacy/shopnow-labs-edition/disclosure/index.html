Disclosure

How this site makes money, stated plainly

This page exists so that nobody has to guess. It is short on purpose.

Updated 2026-11-20Applies to every page
The short version

I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

If you click a product link on this site and buy something, we may earn a commission. The price you pay is the same. Nothing on this site is a paid placement by a brand, and no brand has paid for a position in any guide.

Where the disclosure appears

On every page of this site, in two places:

  • Directly under the site header, before any content, so it is visible without scrolling.
  • Beside every product link, as the literal words Paid link, and again in the footer.

That is deliberate. The FTC's guidance says the closer a disclosure sits to the recommendation the better, and that a disclosure at the very top of a page, outside the article, can be overlooked. Doing both is the only way to satisfy either reading. The wording I get commissions for purchases made through links in this post is the FTC's own example; Paid link is the wording the FTC says should be adequate next to an affiliate link. Terms the FTC says are unclear on their own — such as affiliate link, commissionable link, or a buy now button — are not used as the sole disclosure anywhere.

Why every link carries a tag

Amazon's Associates policies require the Associates ID to appear as a parameter in the URL of each link to an Amazon site. Every outbound product link on this site is a canonical Amazon product address with the tracking ID shopnow047c-20 appended. We do not shorten our own links, because a reader should be able to see where a link goes before clicking it.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish prices. Amazon permits price display only where Amazon serves the link containing the price, or where the figure comes from its API. We have neither, so every price lives on Amazon and may have changed since you last looked. Amazon itself states it cannot confirm an item's price until you order.
  • We do not use Amazon product photography. Listing images are licensed content with rules about caching and alteration. Our product cards are typographic instead.
  • We do not rank by commission. Commission rates played no part in which products appear. The products on this desk were supplied by the site owner.
  • We do not claim to have tested products. We have not. Every specification on this site was read from the manufacturer's listing on the date recorded on the page.
  • We do not offer incentives for clicking a link or buying. Amazon's policies prohibit rewards or discounts in exchange for using Special Links.

Amazon relationship

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. This site is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program. This site is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or otherwise affiliated with Amazon or any brand named on it, and nothing here should be read as an endorsement by Amazon of this site or its content.

One thing still unconfirmed

The tracking ID above is the one supplied by the site owner. Whether the associated Associates account is approved, and whether that tag is registered to this site, could not be confirmed from this environment. It is listed as an open item on the known gaps page rather than being asserted as settled.
