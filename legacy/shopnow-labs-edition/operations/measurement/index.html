Measurement plan

Five events, no cookies, and a collector that is not switched on

Click and conversion measurement was supposed to come first. Here is the instrument, the payloads, and an honest statement that no endpoint exists yet.

Updated 2026-11-20Collector: not configured
Read this first

The site emits the events below to a documented interface. Because no endpoint is configured, every event is dropped before it leaves the browser. Nothing is being recorded, and no figure anywhere on this site is derived from measurement.

The five events

EventFires whenPayloadDeliberately excluded
page_viewEvery page loadpath, referrer category (search / direct / referral / internal), viewport bucketNo IP, no user agent string, no fingerprint
guide_readA guide page reaching 75% scroll depthpath, minutes on page rounded to the minuteNo scroll positions, no keystrokes
product_viewA product page loadproduct id, entry pathNo cross-visit identity
outbound_clickA click on a paid Amazon linkproduct id, page path, link position on pageNo Amazon customer data, no order attribution
capture_submitThe newsletter form being submittedpage path, whether the field validatedNo email address is sent to analytics, ever

Why not just install Google Analytics

You can, and it may be the right answer. It is free, it is capable, and it comes with a consent obligation in several jurisdictions and a third-party script on every page. The design here is the opposite: a small first-party collector that stores aggregate counts and nothing that identifies a person. That choice is not free either — you have to run it.

The decision is recorded as SUG-11 with three paths and a recommendation, so it can be made deliberately rather than by default.

What "conversion" means here

Two different things get called a conversion, and conflating them is how affiliate reporting goes wrong:

  • An outbound click — measured by this site, at the moment someone leaves for Amazon. This site can count it.
  • A qualifying purchase — measured only by Amazon, inside a session that begins with a click on a tagged Special Link and ends at the first of 24 hours, an order, or a click on a different Associates link. Cart purchases can still qualify up to 89 days later. This site cannot count it. C-18

So the site reports clicks. It does not report a conversion rate, because the denominator would be invented. When Amazon reports orders, they go into the campaign ledger with the report date, and never into a score.

What is not measured, and why that is a problem

  • Which question actually brings people in. Needs Search Console (see below) or a search-referrer breakdown from the collector.
  • Whether anyone reaches the paid link. Will be measurable once the collector exists; today the outbound_click event fires into nothing.
  • Whether a click becomes an order. Requires Amazon's report, which requires a working tagged link, which is the open item in FLG-002.

Turning it on

  1. Choose a host for the endpoint and decide the retention period. Recommended: 30 days for raw aggregates, indefinite for daily totals.
  2. Set ANALYTICS_ENDPOINT in assets/js/analytics-config.js. The file ships with an empty string, which is why nothing transmits.
  3. Update the privacy page before switching it on, not after.
  4. Record the switch-on date in the campaign ledger so every later figure has a start date.
  5. Verify the property in Search Console and submit /Affil3/sitemap.xml.
