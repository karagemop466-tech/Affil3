How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

The best-looking appliance is the one you will actually clean and have room for.

By Affil3 editorial team · Published November 20, 2026 · Last reviewed November 20, 2026

Working title. Cleanup claims such as 'dishwasher-safe' come from the manufacturer; check the manual's care section before you rely on them.

Cleanup questions

  • How many removable parts are there, and do they all come out without tools?
  • What does the manufacturer's care section say about washing each part? Write down the exact wording.
  • Is the non-stick surface described with cleaning instructions, such as avoiding metal utensils or abrasive pads? Follow the manufacturer's text.
  • Where will the parts dry?

Counter-space test

  1. Tape the footprint on your counter with painter's tape.
  2. Measure the height with the lid or door fully open, and check the clearance under the cabinets.
  3. Measure how far the door or basket pulls out, and whether anything else on the counter would block it.
  4. Find an outlet and check that the cord reaches without a strain.

Trade-offs to write down

Write a one-line trade-off for each option: for example, 'faster cleanup, less capacity' or 'more functions, more parts to wash'. Writing it down makes the comparison concrete and keeps it honest.
