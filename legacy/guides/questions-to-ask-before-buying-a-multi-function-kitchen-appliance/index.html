Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

Multi-function appliances promise a lot. Ask which functions you will actually use.

By Affil3 editorial team · Published November 20, 2026 · Last reviewed November 20, 2026

Eight questions

  1. Which three functions will you use every week? Ignore the rest when comparing.
  2. Do the presets match the food you cook, or will you set time and temperature yourself?
  3. Can you see the temperature and time while the appliance runs?
  4. Does the manual include time and temperature charts for your foods?
  5. What does the warranty cover, and who services it? Read the warranty text rather than the summary.
  6. Are replacement baskets, racks or trays sold separately? Check the listing and the manufacturer's site.
  7. How loud is it? If the manufacturer gives no figure, note that you could not confirm it.
  8. Where will it live, and how often will you move it?

Why these questions

A list of functions is not the same as a list of things you will use. These questions tie the purchase to your routine and to the documents the manufacturer provides.
