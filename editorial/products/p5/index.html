Suits you if

Knowing when to pull the meat without standing next to the grill.

Skip it if

You want an instant-read thermometer with a screen, or you don't want an app in the loop.

Questions this answers

  • Can I monitor from inside the house?
  • How many pieces of meat can I track at once?
  • Will it survive a hot grill lid?

What you gain

  • Unlimited range because the hub relays over WiFi, not Bluetooth alone.
  • Four internal sensors plus an ambient sensor, so flare-ups show up too.
  • Three probes means a brisket and two sides of the grill at once.
  • Rated to 1,000 F ambient, well past most oven and grill ceilings.

What it costs you

  • The inserted portion of the probe tops out at 212 F — it reads doneness, not oil temperature.
  • You need the app and a paired device; there is no on-probe display.
  • The 70 vs 60 hour battery figure is not consistent across the manufacturer's own pages.
