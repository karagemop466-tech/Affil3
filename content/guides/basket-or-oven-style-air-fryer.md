## The short answer

Most of the work is done by the listing title. Read it as a first filter. A title that says **Basket** or **Dual Basket** describes a drawer-style cooker. A title that says **Oven**, **Toaster Oven** or **French Door** describes a countertop oven with a door. Neither form is better in general. Each fits a different kind of cooking and a different counter.

This guide is editorial. We have not tested these appliances. Everything quoted below is from the listing text on the day we checked it (20 November 2026).

## Questions to answer first

1. **What do you cook most?** Frozen snacks in one tray, or whole meals for several people? A single tray and a full meal ask for different sizes.
2. **How much counter do you have, and what is above it?** Measure depth, width and the height under any cabinet before you open a single listing. Oven-style units with a door need clearance for the door to open.
3. **Do you need two cooking zones?** The first listing (see [the dual basket model]({{link:P01}})) describes "2 Independent 3Qt Nonstick Dishwasher Safe Frying Baskets". Two baskets help if you want two foods on two timers. They do not add cooking space to a single pan.
4. **Do you need more than frying?** The French-door option (see [the toaster oven model]({{link:P02}})) lists "24 One-Touch Preset Options" and "Six Cooking Accessories Included" in its title. Presets are a count, not a promise. Check what each preset sets before you rely on it.
5. **Where will it live when it is not in use?** A basket that nests can hide in a cupboard. A countertop oven usually stays out.

## What to compare on the listing

- **Form.** Basket, dual basket, or oven, as stated in the title.
- **Capacity wording.** Titles mix total quarts ("6 Quart", "26QT", "8 Qt") with per-basket figures ("3Qt" per basket). Work out which number you are comparing before you compare numbers. See the [capacity checklist](@/guides/air-fryer-capacity-checklist/).
- **Cleanup claims.** "Dishwasher Safe" and "Dishwasher-Safe" appear in the titles. Check the manual for which parts are safe to wash this way.
- **Heat claims.** One title lists a "450°F Hi-Fry Option". Read what the manual says about maximum temperature for each mode.

## What the listing cannot tell you

- How evenly it browns food in your kitchen.
- How loud it is, how hot the outside gets, or how long it takes to clean.
- Whether the price is right today. Prices are not shown on this site under Amazon's terms.
- What other buyers think. Star ratings are not shown here for the same reason.
