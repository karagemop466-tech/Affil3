## The required statement

**As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.**

This sentence appears under the masthead and in the footer of every page on this site, in text, because that is what Amazon's Associates Program Operating Agreement requires - "clearly and prominently" - and it is not something we place behind a link or a button.

## What that means when you read an article here

- Links to Amazon product pages are **affiliate links**, marked `Sponsored` where you see them.
- If you click one and buy something on Amazon, we may earn a commission. It does not change what you pay.
- Amazon's program assigns the purchase to us through a session that starts with your click. The window is short, which is one reason we do not try to shorten your path with redirects or link cloakers: you should be able to see where a link goes before you click it.

## What we do *not* claim

- We are not endorsed by, sponsored by, or in any partnership with Amazon beyond enrollment in the Associates Program.
- We have no relationship with the brands listed here. No product was sent to us, no brand reviewed our copy, and no brand saw this site before you did.
- We do not run sponsored posts, paid placements, native advertising, or brand-funded guides. Nothing on this site was written for money from a manufacturer, and there is no route by which that could happen quietly, because the pages are generated from a register that has to name its sources.
- We do not present star ratings, review counts, prices, or availability, and we do not restate a manufacturer's numbers as our own findings.

## The link-level version

Next to every outbound commerce link you will see a short line in the same shape as this one: *"Sponsored - we may earn a commission if you buy after clicking."* That is the link-level disclosure, sitting where the click happens rather than on a page you would have to go looking for.

## Advertising and cookies

This site serves no advertising, sets no cookies, and loads no third-party scripts. See [Privacy](/privacy/) for the one thing we may measure and how it is done without identifying you.

## Questions

Program questions, corrections, or a request for how a figure was verified: use the register. Every claim on the site has a source row with an access date and, where it matters, the quoted text. If we cannot show you the source, the site already says so out loud on that page.
