## Amazon Associates statement

> As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

This is the statement required by the Associates Program Operating Agreement, Section 5 ([SRC-01]({{link:SRC-01}})). It appears under the masthead on every page and again in the footer.

## What we do with links

- Every link to an Amazon listing is marked `rel="sponsored"`, which is how Google asks sites to mark paid or affiliate links ([SRC-06]({{link:SRC-06}})).
- If you buy through one of these links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commission rates are not shown here, because we have not been able to read them from Amazon's schedule (see [FLG-010](@/flagged-for-review/)).
- Some listings have not been confirmed yet. Those are labelled on each card and in the [product register](@/products/).

## What we do not do

- **We have not tested these products.** Guides describe listing text and public guidance. Under FTC guidance, you can only describe experience you have had ([SRC-05]({{link:SRC-05}})).
- **We do not show prices or star ratings.** Amazon's policies limit showing prices and ratings to approved data sources ([SRC-02]({{link:SRC-02}}), 2(b) and 6(t)). The site does not use them.
- **We do not use Amazon product photos.**

## Other relationships

The brief did not say whether the site owner has any other relationship with these products, such as free items, payments or discounts. Until the owner answers, this is an open item: [FLG-014](@/flagged-for-review/). If there is such a relationship, it must be disclosed next to the product it concerns ([SRC-05]({{link:SRC-05}}) says to place it where it is hard to miss).

## Where the disclosure sits

FTC guidance says disclosures are likely to be missed if they appear only on an About page, behind a "more" link, or mixed into a group of links ([SRC-05]({{link:SRC-05}})). This site places the statement in the masthead area above the first product on every page, and in the footer.
