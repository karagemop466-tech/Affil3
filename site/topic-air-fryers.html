The axes that decide it

#AxisWhy it decides the purchaseWhere a buyer checks it
1Capacity you can useAdvertised quarts measure the cavity, not what fits in one batch; a 6-quart basket still cooks a single layer of wings.the listing's own capacity statement plus the internal dimensions if published
2Footprint and clearanceThe appliance has to live somewhere with vent clearance, and the counter is the scarce resource, not the outlet.measured dimensions on the listing, a tape measure, and your own counter
