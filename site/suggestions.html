SG-02 · Replace the supplied short links with generated, validated Special Links before anything is clickable

adopted-in-build P0

Problem
Five links arrive as https://link.amazon/<9 chars>. Their destination, their tag-preservation and their existence are all unconfirmed, and both the redirect rule and the formatting rule can void a commission.
Proposal
