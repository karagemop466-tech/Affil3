Known and unknown

What has to be true before a decision here is reasonable

Three states only: known (with the official basis), discoverable (with the exact action that finds it), and needs you (a decision or a credential nobody in this sandbox can supply). Each open row carries the discovery method, so "we do not know" is never the end of a thread, and a blank answer is never quietly upgraded into a guess.

Known — with the basis

— What disclosure sentence is mandatory on the site? known

answer
"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.", clearly and prominently.
basis
Operating Agreement Section 5, verbatim [S01]
consequence
Already implemented as the strip under the masthead and the footer repeat. Changing it is an agreement breach, not a design choice.

— What must every outbound product link contain? known

answer
The tag parameter, in the form XXXXX-##, on a link to an Amazon Site, accessed directly from the site, in a link format Amazon provides.
basis
Participation Requirements 2(a)-2(b) [S02] [S03]
consequence
tools/build_site.py generates links only in that form, and check_site.py fails the build if the tag is absent or differs from the registered tracking ID.

— Can the site display prices, star ratings or review quotes that came from anywhere except a live Amazon-served link or an approved API call? known

answer
No. Not from a cached spreadsheet, not from a search snippet, not from a memory of the ASIN.
basis
Participation Requirements 2(b), 6(t) [S06] [S07]
consequence
There is no price column and no rating column anywhere on the site, by design, until API access is confirmed. This is why the tables look sparse.

— Can we scrape Amazon listing pages to populate product data? known

answer
No, on two independent grounds: the licence excludes robots and data-mining tools, and Program Content may not be aggregated, analysed, extracted or repurposed without express prior written approval.
basis
IP License 1 and 2(e) [S10] [S14]
consequence
Nothing in tools/ makes an HTTP request to Amazon. Any future 'research agent' must either use the licensed API or run on data we create ourselves.

— Can Amazon product data be used to train or fine-tune a model, including our own content generator? known

answer
Not without express prior written approval; the same clause also covers Special Links.
basis
IP License 1 final paragraph; Participation Requirements 5 [S10]
consequence
The generator in tools/ composes from our own data files and human-written guide bodies only. No fine-tuning on Program Content is attempted anywhere in this repo.

— How long is a click worth, and what does that mean for reporting? known

answer
The session window closes 24 hours after the click, an order completed within 89 days of the click still counts if it was added to cart, and the item must ship and be paid for within 180 days of purchase.
basis
Commission Income Statement 1(b)-(c) [S18]
consequence
Clicks and orders are reported as separate, non-adjacent columns in the ledger and on the measurement page. Any 'clicks this week x assumed rate' figure would be fiction.

— Can we validate tracking by making a test purchase ourselves? known

answer
No. You may not purchase through your own Special Links, or have friends/family/contractors do it, for any reason including testing.
basis
Participation Requirements 6(u) [S12]
consequence
The link test in docs/LINK_TEST_PROTOCOL.md verifies destination and the tag in the address bar, and otherwise waits for a real reader's click to show in reports. The protocol says 'do not buy' in its first line.

— Can the site offer 'notify me when the price drops' or a price-history chart? known

answer
No. Price tracking and price alerting functionality is prohibited unless otherwise agreed by Amazon.
basis
Participation Requirements 6(y) [S13]
consequence
Rejected as a feature (SG-11) rather than deferred. 'Price alert' strings are grepped for by the compliance gate and fail the build.

— How long may product data and images be stored locally? known

answer
Images: not storable at all, only a link to the image, for up to 24 hours. Other Product Advertising Content: cacheable up to 24 hours, then it must be refreshed and re-displayed. ASINs: indefinitely.
basis
IP License 2(h) [S08]
consequence
The site uses original illustrations and ASIN strings; data_shelf_life in program.json encodes the 24h rule and the build warns when a non-null product field is older than 24 hours.

Discoverable — here is how

P0 What is the account's application date, and how many qualifying sales are on the board? needs answer

why it blocks
The 180-day/3-sale rule decides whether there is any runway left to spend on content before the account is withdrawn [S17].
who can answer
Account holder
how to discover it
Associates Central home page states the deadline and the count in plain text. Record it: python3 tools/campaign_ledger.py program --application-date YYYY-MM-DD --qualifying-sales N
record it as
{"application_date": "", "qualifying_sales": null, "window_state": ""}

P0 Is `shopnow047c-20` a tracking ID that exists on this account, and is this site's URL listed among the registered websites? needs answer

why it blocks
Links tagged with a tracking ID that is not on the account, or from an unregistered site, will not report - and an unregistered site is exactly what the reporting rules assume is registered.
who can answer
Account holder
how to discover it
Associates Central > Account Settings > Manage your tracking IDs, and the website list on the same account page. Compare character-for-character with data/program.json.
record it as
{"tracking_id_exists": null, "registered_site_url": "", "marketplace": ""}

P0 What do the five submitted short links actually resolve to, and what is each product's real title, brand and category? needs answer

why it blocks
Every shelf card, guide reference and cluster assignment depends on it (FLG-002, FLG-005).
who can answer
Anyone with a browser
how to discover it
Open each link in a signed-out browser window and read the final URL and the H1; or search the 9-character string on amazon.com and see whether any listing matches. Record with: python3 tools/product_intake.py confirm --slot N --observed-redirect '<url>' --title '<exact listing title>' --method 'manual click' --date YYYY-MM-DD
record it as
{"observed_redirect_target": "", "product_name": "", "brand": "", "category_from_listing": ""}

P0 Do we have Creators API or PA API access, and which operations does the granted licence permit? needs answer

why it blocks
It decides whether prices, availability, ratings and images can ever appear on the site, and therefore whether comparison tables are buildable at all [S06] [S07].
who can answer
Account holder, from the Associates Central API tab
how to discover it
Check the account for API credentials; read the licence grant screen. If credentials exist, one ItemsApi call against a confirmed ASIN settles it.
record it as
{"api_available": null, "credentials_present": null, "operations_granted": [], "note": ""}

P1 Which product attributes can we lawfully state, and from where? needs answer

why it blocks
Everything a buyer calls 'useful' in a comparison — capacity in quarts, wattage, footprint, dishwasher-safe basket — has to come from a citable source, and a third-party spec sheet is not one we are allowed to cache.
who can answer
Editorial, with a source decision
how to discover it
Three permitted routes: (1) text we write from our own measurement, (2) a link to the manufacturer's published specification page, (3) fresh API-served content within its 24h window. Pick the route per field and record it in the guide's claims table.
record it as
{"route_per_field": {}, "reviewer": "", "approved_date": ""}

P1 Is hands-on testing feasible — do we own or can we borrow the units, and on what schedule? needs answer

why it blocks
Original testing is the only durable defence against the 'thin affiliate' classification and the only honest basis for any recommendation sentence [S27].
who can answer
Account holder / budget owner
how to discover it
Answer yes/no per product. If no, the site's editorial voice must stay question-shaped and checklist-shaped (as it does now) and must never say 'we tested'.
record it as
{"units_available": [], "test_window_days": null, "budget": null}

Needs the owner

P0 What is the intended conversion measurement, given that a test purchase is prohibited and self-reported revenue is not available? needs owner decision

why it blocks
It decides what the ledger can ever contain, and therefore whether 'optimise campaigns' is a real instruction or a slogan [S12] [S18].
who can answer
Account holder, plus reading one report screen
how to discover it
Decide: do you accept 'clicks measured locally, orders transcribed from Associates reports with a dated provenance line' as the ceiling, or do you want to apply for API-based reporting access too?
record it as
{"accepted_definition_of_conversion": "", "report_cadence": "", "who_transcribes": ""}

P1 Does disclosure placement meet the FTC standard you want to be judged against? needs owner decision

why it blocks
Amazon's rule is 'clear and prominent'; the FTC's standard is a disclosure a significant minority would not miss, close to the recommendation. We implemented the stricter reading, but the acceptable floor is your risk appetite [S19] [S20] [S21].
who can answer
Owner, optionally with counsel
how to discover it
Look at the top of any guide on a phone, without scrolling. If the strip is not the first thing you register, we tighten it. Legal sign-off is only needed if you want to relax placement, not to keep it.
record it as
{"placement_approved": null, "reviewed_on_mobile": null, "notes": ""}

P0 Which niche do we actually own — air fryers, or kitchen appliances broadly, or something else entirely? needs owner decision

why it blocks
Niche decides the cluster plan, the competitive set, and whether the five products even belong on the same shelf. The brief supplied topics as working titles only.
who can answer
Owner
how to discover it
One hour: list 10 sites ranking for 'best air fryer' and note how many are hands-on. If most are, we either find a sub-niche with real testing or pick a different category where the shelf is thinner. Record the decision in SG-04.
record it as
{"chosen_niche": "", "sub_niche": "", "rejected": [], "rationale": ""}

P1 Who is the byline, and does that person exist and consent to being named? needs owner decision

why it blocks
Authorship is the trust signal the brief asked for, and the FTC's review-solicitation guidance plus Amazon's anti-manipulation stance both make a fake reviewer identity a liability rather than a decoration.
who can answer
Owner
how to discover it
Name a real person or a real, described collective ('Counter Tested is written by two home cooks; no laboratory') and edit data/people.json. The build refuses to render a bio longer than what is true.
record it as
{"author_name": "", "author_basis_for_advice": "", "contact_channel": ""}

P1 Stay on GitHub Pages, or move to a host where one small process can run? needs owner decision

why it blocks
On Pages there is no collector endpoint, no form handling and no rewrite rules, so measurement, reader capture and any personalisation are capped until this is decided (FLG-009).
who can answer
Owner
how to discover it
Cost a custom domain (about $10-15/yr at a mainstream registrar) and one small host against the value of measuring clicks server-side. Decide, then either point DNS at Pages or run tools/collect_server.py on the host and set measurement.endpoint.
record it as
{"decision": "", "domain": "", "host_for_endpoint": "", "budget_monthly": null}

P0 What is the policy on the identifier gap — publish the shelf as-is, or hold publication until the five links resolve? needs owner decision

why it blocks
It decides whether this merge ships links at all, and how the front page reads for the next few weeks.
who can answer
Owner
how to discover it
Current behaviour is the middle path: content published, links gated. The alternatives are (a) publish live /dp/ links now without confirmation, or (b) no shelf until every identifier is confirmed. Each is one flag change; the trade-off is honesty-to-readers versus time-to-clicks.
record it as
{"policy": "gated (current)", "alternatives_considered": [], "review_date": ""}

Suggestions for discovering the missing information

The methods above are the short list. Beyond them: (1) one browser session inside the Associates account settles Q-01, Q-02, Q-03 and Q-04 in about 25 minutes and is the highest-value hour available to this project; (2) Search Console verification (SG-29) is the only route to Q-13, and no on-site instrument substitutes for it; (3) a paper clip and a tape measure answer Q-06; (4) asking Amazon Associates support in writing whether a planned analytical use of API data needs approval converts FLG-004 from an interpretation into a documented position — we have not sent that request, so do not treat it as done.
