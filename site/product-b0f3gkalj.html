Flags attached to this listing

FLG-002

Every submitted URL is https://link.amazon/<identifier> where <identifier> is 9 characters: B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj. Public examples of an item identifier on Amazon are 10 characters, and Amazon's own policy text refers to 'Amazon Standard Identification Numbers (ASINs)' without publishing a length definition that this session could retrieve.

FLG-003

Two primary clauses bear on it and neither settles it. Participation Requirements 6(w) prohibits using a link shortening service 'in a manner that makes it unclear that you are linking to an Amazon Site' - a presentation test, not a format ban. Commission Income Statement 2(e) disqualifies purchases arriving through a 'Redirecting Link' that sends users indirectly to Amazon via an intermediate site without an affirmative click there - a tracking test. Whether link.amazon is (i) an Amazon-owned short-link surface that preserves the tag, (ii) an unrelated intermediate, or (iii) nothing at all, cannot be determined without clicking it from a browser.

FLG-005

The only category signal in the input was the suggested editorial topics ('Basket or oven-style air fryer...', 'air fryer capacity', 'multi-function kitchen appliance'), which the brief itself labels as 'working titles - not claims about popularity, search volume or product superiority'.
