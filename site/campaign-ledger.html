Evidence, later

Campaign ledger

An empty ledger with a fixed schema, on purpose. Rows arrive only from authorised first-party reports, and only with the dated provenance attached, so that any future claim about performance can be walked back to a screen somebody read. There is no conversion score, no revenue forecast and no projection on this page, because there is nothing to compute them from.

data/ledger/campaign.jsonl · 0 rows
timestampsourcereport datechanneltracking ideligible sessionsclicksreported orders
No rows. This is the correct state: there are no authorised first-party report numbers to transcribe yet, and the tool refuses to invent them.

How a row gets added

python3 tools/campaign_ledger.py append \ --source 'Associates Central > Reports > Tracking report' \ --report-date 2026-12-14 --channel organic \ --eligible-sessions 12 --clicks 31 --reported-orders 2

The tool rejects a row with no --source or no --report-date, rejects unknown field names, and never writes a computed percentage into the file.

Why the columns are the columns

The brief specified timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks and reported orders. Two facts from the programme explain why "and that is all we can honestly say": the commission session closes 24 hours after the click, an order completed up to 89 days later still counts if it came from the cart, and payment/dispatch can lag up to 180 days after purchase. So a clicks-to-orders ratio computed on this week's numbers is a fiction about a window that has not closed. [S18]
