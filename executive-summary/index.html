What is built

  • A publication-style site with a products-first front page, a buyer-actionable shop table, a side-by-side comparison page, 3 category clusters, 18 question-led guides, a Journal of 7 articles tied to the products, a three-question fit check at /find/, disclosed editorial imagery, an RSS feed and a sitemap.
  • A decision board at /decisions/: reviewable suggestions with options and a recommendation, plus the questions that must be answered before any decision, and the assembled design prompt the suggestions feed into.
  • Dashboards for sources, questions, suggestions, flagged items, the campaign ledger (empty), measurement, compliance, the publishing workflow, and a privacy notice. All are linked from the footer.
  • A disclosure strip under the masthead on every page, repeated in the footer, using the statement required by Operating Agreement §5, and an explicit index,follow robots meta tag on every root page.
  • Four dated verification rounds are on file in data/observations/. The most recent re-read seven official pages first-hand, recorded the fetches it could not complete as failures rather than as agreement, corrected eleven mis-citations, and added the gate rules that catch that class (FLG-044). The same round corrected a findability claim this site had been making about itself: a robots.txt file manages crawling and neither excludes a page from Google's index nor carries a noindex rule - Google's own wording (S24, S26), recorded as FLG-043.
  • A privacy-preserving click counter in the browser and a self-hostable collector. The collector is inactive on the live site until an endpoint is set (FLG-009).
  • Automated checks (tools/verify.mjs) for disclosure presence, sponsored attributes, tags, sources, sitemap and feed coverage, and internal links, plus a CI workflow that re-runs the build and the gate on every push.

What is verified

  • The Associates disclosure statement, the Special Link and tag rules, and the anti-cloaking clause are read from the official Associates pages (S01, S02).
  • The rel="sponsored" guidance (S06), FTC disclosure guidance (S07, S08), and USDA temperatures (S09, S10) are read from official pages.
  • For each product, the observed Amazon ASIN, title and breadcrumb are read from the amazon.com page the link resolved to on 2026-11-20 (S04, S05).
  • An Amazon-owned ASIN definition has been located in Associates Central help (S12/S13, excerpt; manual re-check pending, FLG-012).
  • The ThermoMaven manufacturer page was located on 2026-11-20 (S21). It states a 1-second instant read, NIST-certified ±0.5 °F accuracy, a 100% waterproof design and a lifetime warranty, matching the listing title feature for feature. The row now carries a sourced specification set, labelled as family-page figures until the listing's model field confirms the F1 Ace (FLG-025, Q-14); its purchase link is withheld pending G-02.

What is not verified, and why

  • The intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date and source are blank for all five products. The submitted tokens are 9 characters, and the observed destinations differ from the tokens (FLG-001, FLG-002).
  • Two destinations were transposed in an earlier register revision and corrected after a retest, and one link first hit an intermittent checkpoint page before resolving (FLG-004, FLG-022, FLG-028). Manufacturer-page cross-checks added FLG-023 to FLG-027. Two of those were themselves corrected in a later pass: the ThermoMaven unit does have a maker page, which had been missed (FLG-025), and the Chefman 8 Qt conflict had been measured against the wrong Chefman page and is really a wattage and case-size difference (FLG-026). A 2026-11-20 re-fetch of two short links returned the Amazon home page rather than a product page, which is the same intermittent behaviour and does not establish that any link is dead (FLG-031). The status of the supplied short links is unresolved, not invalid (FLG-003).
  • No Special Link has been tested in Associates Central. The sandbox has no Associates login (FLG-005, gate G-02).
  • No prices, ratings, sales or commission figures are shown. The campaign ledger is empty and no conversion score or revenue forecast is presented.
  • Several reader-facing editions of this project are published under one domain. Only this root publication is in the sitemap; the others are excluded from the index and listed on the archive page (FLG-032, Q-15, S-11).

Evidence needed before expanding

  1. Use the original enrolled link-generation record or campaign brief to confirm intended products separately from observed redirects (Q-01).
  2. Confirm the account declares this site and that tracking ID shopnow047c-20 is on the account (Q-03, FLG-011).
  3. Run gate G-02 in Associates Central for each product. Record the destination and tracking report separately from site click data (Q-02).
  4. Choose options on the Suggestions page. Nothing there is implemented until you choose.

Measure before expanding

Use Google Search Console for queries and impressions, the site collector for outbound clicks, and Amazon Associates reports (S02) for reported orders. A site click is not an order. Compare dated reporting windows, and leave the ledger empty until authorized data is available.

Limits

This build cannot pull Amazon product data, prices or sales reports. Those require authorized access (for example the Product Advertising API or Associates reports), which the sandbox does not have. The site does not scrape Amazon.
