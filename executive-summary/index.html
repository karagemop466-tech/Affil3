Executive summary · November 20, 2026

Where this stands

What is built

  • A publication-style site with a front page led by the five submitted products, a shop table, four category clusters, five question-led guides, and an RSS feed and sitemap.
  • Dashboards for sources, questions, suggestions, flagged items, the campaign ledger (empty), measurement, and compliance. All are linked from the footer.
  • A disclosure strip under the masthead on every page, repeated in the footer, using the statement required by Operating Agreement §5.
  • A privacy-preserving click counter in the browser and a self-hostable collector. The collector is inactive on the live site until an endpoint is set (FLG-009).
  • Automated checks (tools/verify.mjs) for disclosure presence, sponsored attributes, tags, sources, sitemap and feed coverage, and internal links.

What is verified

  • The Associates disclosure statement, the Special Link and tag rules, and the anti-cloaking clause are read from the official Associates pages (S01, S02).
  • The rel="sponsored" guidance (S06), FTC disclosure guidance (S07, S08), and USDA temperatures (S09, S10) are read from official pages.
  • For each product, the observed Amazon ASIN, title and breadcrumb are read from the amazon.com page the link resolved to on 2026-11-20 (S04, S05).

What is not verified, and why

  • The intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date and source are blank for all five products. The submitted tokens are 9 characters, and the observed destinations differ from the tokens (FLG-001, FLG-002).
  • All 5 submitted links resolve to an Amazon product page, confirmed by three independent reads. What is not confirmed is that any resolved product is the one intended: intent is a property of the account that issued the link, not of the page it lands on (FLG-002). None of the resolved URLs carries a tag parameter (FLG-003). An earlier reading of one link as a checkpoint page was an intermittent bot check and is recorded as resolved (FLG-004, FLG-022).
  • No Special Link has been tested in Associates Central. The sandbox has no Associates login (FLG-005, gate G-02).
  • No prices, ratings, sales or commission figures are shown. The campaign ledger is empty and no conversion score or revenue forecast is presented.

Decisions needed from you

  1. Give the intended product for each short link, or confirm the observed product (Q-01).
  2. Confirm the account declares this site and that tracking ID shopnow047c-20 is on the account (Q-03, FLG-011).
  3. Run gate G-02: one Special Link per product in Associates Central, then tell us the result (Q-04).
  4. Choose options on the Suggestions page. Nothing there is implemented until you choose.

Limits

This build cannot pull Amazon product data, prices or sales reports. Those require authorized access (for example the Product Advertising API or Associates reports), which the sandbox does not have. The site does not scrape Amazon.
