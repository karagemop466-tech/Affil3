What was verified against a primary source

  • Every specification on every product page was read on the manufacturer's own page on 2026-11-20 and is cited by source id (S14–S18). Where the Amazon listing disagrees with the maker, both are shown and the disagreement is flagged (FLG-023 to FLG-026).
  • Three listing-versus-maker conflicts found: P01 is called an "oven" in the listing but is a two-drawer basket fryer; P03's listing advertises 450 °F against the maker's 400 °F; P04's "NIST-certified" claim has no certificate behind it and NIST's own FAQ says it does not endorse products.
  • The Associates and FTC rules were read from Amazon's Operating Agreement and Program Policies and from the FTC and eCFR pages (S01, S02, S07, S08). The disclosure statement, the sponsored attribute and the tracking tag are enforced by an automated gate that runs on every build (tools/verify.mjs, 2,171 checks).
  • USDA safe temperatures for the thermometer guides were read from the FSIS chart (S09, last updated April 14, 2025).

What is not verified, and why

  • The intended product behind each submitted link. The five short links are 9-character tokens; every Amazon product ID is 10 characters. Each link redirected to a product page, and that destination is recorded separately from the "intended" fields, which stay blank (FLG-001, FLG-002).
  • Whether any link earns a commission. No Special Link has been generated or tested in Associates Central, so no destination or tracking report has been confirmed (FLG-005, gate G-02).
  • Whether anything converts. The campaign ledger is empty and analytics is switched off until an endpoint exists (FLG-009). No conversion score or revenue forecast appears anywhere on this site, because none could be computed from data we hold.
  • Whether anyone has used these products. Nobody has. No page makes a hands-on claim (FLG-033).

What only you can decide

  1. Confirm the intended product for each of the five links (Q-01), or accept the observed destination as the intended one.
  2. Run gate G-02: generate one Special Link per product in Associates Central, click it, and confirm the destination and the tracking report (Q-04).
  3. Work the suggestions board: 17 decisions, each with a recommendation and a cost. The four that change the most are imagery (S-05), analytics (S-03), the second site at /editorial/ (S-16) and what to publish next (S-17).
  4. Say whether any product was actually used (Q-14). This is the single biggest limit on how persuasive the copy can be.

Limits of this build

No Amazon product data, prices or earnings reports were pulled. Those need the Product Advertising API or Associates reports, which require an approved account and are not available in this session. The site does not scrape Amazon. Prices and ratings are deliberately not displayed: they change hourly, and Amazon's terms restrict how they may be shown (FLG-031).
