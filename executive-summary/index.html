Executive summary · November 20, 2026

Where this stands

What is built

  • A publication-style site with a products-first front page, a shop table, three category clusters, five question-led guides, disclosed editorial imagery, an RSS feed and a sitemap.
  • Dashboards for sources, questions, suggestions, flagged items, the campaign ledger (empty), measurement, compliance, the publishing workflow, and a privacy notice. All are linked from the footer.
  • One live, indexable publication. Four earlier parallel editions were archived under legacy/editions/ with their builders, data and tests intact rather than deleted, and robots.txt disallows /legacy/ so search engines see a single site (S-01, option D).
  • A disclosure strip under the masthead on every page, repeated in the footer, using the statement required by Operating Agreement §5.
  • A privacy-preserving click counter in the browser and a self-hostable collector. The collector is inactive on the live site until an endpoint is set (FLG-009).
  • Automated checks (tools/verify.mjs) for disclosure presence, sponsored attributes, tags, sources, sitemap and feed coverage, and internal links, plus a CI workflow that re-runs the build and the gate on every push.

What is verified

Every source below was fetched in full on November 20, 2026, and the source register records how and when. A gate in tools/verify.mjs refuses any source that does not.

  • Amazon's own rules. The Operating Agreement statement requirement, the Special Link and tag rules, the anti-cloaking clause, and the Participation Requirements are read from the official Associates pages (S01, S02, S03, S17).
  • Amazon's own definitions. Associates Central help defines an ASIN as “the 10-digit code that Amazon uses to denote specific products” and documents the simple text link format (S18). This settles FLG-001: the five submitted tokens are 9 characters, so none is an ASIN.
  • Amazon's stated bar for a site. The application review help page states the qualified-sales requirement, the public-availability requirement, the “at least 10 posts” content rule of thumb, and the 60-day recency expectation (S19).
  • Disclosure law and search guidance. The FTC endorsement guides (S07, S08, including the affiliate-link example at 16 CFR 255.5 Example 11), Google's outbound-link and spam policies (S06, S09), and the USDA temperature guidance (S10, S20) are read from the official pages.
  • Manufacturer specifications. Every published specification is read from the maker's own page (S11–S15) or, for the one unit with no maker page, withheld entirely (FLG-025). Where the listing and the maker disagree, both figures are shown side by side (FLG-024, FLG-026).
  • The observed listings. All five observed ASINs were re-read directly on November 20, 2026, and each page's own title matches the title in the register (see the dated observation record).

What is not verified, and why

  • Which product was meant. The intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date and source are blank for all five products. The tokens are not ASINs (FLG-001), and the token-to-listing mapping did not reproduce on the last check — two links reached the Amazon home page and three were unreachable from the check environment (FLG-031). It is unverified, not disproven.
  • Whether a click earns anything. No Special Link has been tested in Associates Central, so tag attribution is unproven (FLG-005, gate G-02). The status of the supplied short links is unresolved, not invalid (FLG-003).
  • Content volume. Amazon describes a rule of thumb of at least 10 posts; this site publishes five guides (FLG-032).
  • Claim stability. Two destinations were transposed in an earlier register revision and corrected after a retest (FLG-022). Manufacturer-page cross-checks produced FLG-023 to FLG-027, including one tracked unit whose listing and maker page describe two different machines (FLG-026).
  • No Special Link has been tested in Associates Central. The sandbox has no Associates login (FLG-005, gate G-02).
  • No prices, ratings, sales or commission figures are shown. The campaign ledger is empty and no conversion score or revenue forecast is presented.

Evidence needed before expanding

  1. Use the original enrolled link-generation record or campaign brief to confirm intended products separately from observed redirects (Q-01).
  2. Confirm the account declares this site and that tracking ID shopnow047c-20 is on the account (Q-03, FLG-011).
  3. Run gate G-02 in Associates Central for each product. Record the destination and tracking report separately from site click data (Q-04).
  4. Choose options on the Suggestions page. Nine decisions are still open; S-01 is settled and recorded as implemented.

Measure before expanding

Use Google Search Console for queries and impressions, the site collector for outbound clicks, and Amazon Associates reports (S02) for reported orders. A site click is not an order. Compare dated reporting windows, and leave the ledger empty until authorized data is available.

Limits

This build cannot pull Amazon product data, prices or sales reports. Those require authorized access (for example the Product Advertising API or Associates reports), which the sandbox does not have. The site does not scrape Amazon.
