How to read the status column

  • verified: the full page was retrieved and read on the date shown.
  • excerpt: only a search-result excerpt of the official page was available. Re-check the exact wording before relying on it.
  • observed: a value read off a live Amazon page on a dated fetch. Useful as evidence of what happened that day, and capable of changing tomorrow.
