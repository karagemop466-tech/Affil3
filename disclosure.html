Affiliate disclosure

Amazon Associates statement

As an Amazon Associate, Affil3 earns from qualifying purchases. This site participates in the Amazon Associates program. When you click an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, the site may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Where the disclosure appears

  • A disclosure strip appears under the masthead, above the fold, on every page of this site.
  • The same disclosure is repeated in the footer of every page.
  • This full disclosure statement is available at /disclosure.html and linked from every disclosure strip.

How affiliate links are marked

  • Every outbound product link on this site carries rel="sponsored" (Amazon affiliate links).
  • Links are kept exactly as submitted; no submitted short link is claimed to be invalid, and no link has been replaced with a "Special Link" until it has been tested in the enrolled Associates workflow (see project note S02).

Tracking ID

The selected Associates tracking ID submitted for this project is shopnow047c-20. This ID has not been confirmed against the Amazon Associates dashboard in this environment (see flagged item FLG-001). It is stated here for transparency only.

Editorial independence

Guides are written around buyer questions, not product payouts. No guide on this site claims that any product is "best," top-rated, or superior. Working titles are editorial hypotheses, not performance claims.

Standards referenced

  • FTC Endorsement Guides — clear and conspicuous disclosure of material connections: FTC endorsement guides.
  • FTC .com Disclosures — how to make effective disclosures in digital advertising: FTC .com disclosures.
