Method

How we choose and check

The rules every product row and every guide on this site must pass.

What gets listed

Only appliances the site's owner submitted. We do not add products from our own research, and we do not rank or score them, because we hold no authorized performance data. Five rows, each with its submitted link, its observed Amazon destination, and its manufacturer specification sources.

How each row is checked

  1. Observe the listing. Each submitted short link is fetched and the final Amazon page recorded (title, breadcrumb, /dp/ identifier). Observations are dated and never copied into the intended-product fields.
  2. Check the maker. Where the manufacturer publishes a specification page, its figures populate the product page's specification grid. Where the maker is silent, the cell reads not stated — never filled in.
  3. Record conflicts. Where the listing and the maker disagree, both readings are published side by side and the row is flagged (see FLG-023 through FLG-027 on the flag page).
  4. Withhold what we cannot stand behind. No prices, ratings, availability, or hands-on test claims. If a maker publishes no specification page at all, every specification is withheld (FLG-025).
  5. Link everything. Every row and guide carries its source IDs, linked in the source register.

What would change a row

A confirmation from the owner about the intended product behind each link (Q-01), or a manufacturer page appearing for a model we could not verify. Corrections are logged on the Flagged for review page with their dates.
