Findings & analysis

What the verification and observation passes found, in plain language — what it means, and what each finding changes about the next decision. No speculation fills the gaps; the gaps are listed on Flagged for review.

Finding 1 — The niche signal is strong and consistent

Observation

  • Four of five submitted links resolved to air-fryer listings (dual-basket, French-door oven-style, family-size basket) and one to a wireless meat thermometer, all under Home & Kitchen. The brief's four editorial hypotheses are all air-fryer/kitchen questions.

What it means

  • The working niche — air fryers and multi-function kitchen appliances — is supported by the observations, and the four published guides align with the shortlist's formats.
  • Caveat: observed landing ≠ intended product until Q-03 is answered, so the niche plan stays a flagged hypothesis, not a fact (FLG-005).

Finding 2 — The tracking gap is the single most important issue

Observation

  • None of the observed landing URLs contained tag=shopnow047c-20. Landing URLs carried lv=shuf / btn_ref=srctok-… parameters instead.
  • Commission requires the qualifying-purchase chain: a click through a Special Link [S01, S03].

What it means

  • Until gate G5 (one-link test in the enrolled Associates workflow, destination + tracking report confirmed) passes, every click through the public CTAs may be unattributed — which would make any conversion measurement meaningless and any optimization premature.
  • That is exactly why this session: (a) records the observation without a verdict (FLG-003's no-verdict rule), (b) ships the kill switch, and (c) refuses to present any conversion or revenue figures.

Finding 3 — Identifier mismatch: intent ≠ observation

Observation

  • Submitted path identifiers are 9 characters (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj); Amazon's official ASIN definition is 10 characters [S05].
  • Every observed landing ASIN (10 chars) differs from its submitted path identifier: B04G1MEwY→B0CDY277D3 · B02NbDxp3→B09B7SB46R · B00hJ57DV→B08DKYBTPH · B0f3GKalj→B0DZFD6R9Z · B0c8moW59→unresolved.

What it means

  • The two-never-merge rule exists precisely for this: intent (what the submitter meant) and observation (what the link did, once, from one vantage point) are different claims about the world, and only the second was measurable this session.
  • The reconciliation — which listing is "the product" for each link — needs the account-side test (Q-01/Q-03), not more observation from outside.

Finding 4 — The automation stack's official path changed under our feet

Verification

  • PA-API 5.0 is deprecated; the Creators API is the supported successor [S12, S13], gated on Associates enrollment plus 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days.

What it means

  • Any "AI-powered product research/automation" ambition (the brief's platform vision) must be planned against the Creators API, and its real-world prerequisite is sales volume — not engineering. The pragmatic sequencing: verify links manually first (G5), measure, hit the API threshold, then automate pricing/spec refresh.
