What was observed, not verified

  • All five link.amazon URLs resolved server-side on 2026-11-20: four to amazon.com product pages, one to an Amazon checkpoint interstitial. Observed targets are recorded per product in separate observation fields that are never merged with intent (confirmation records).
  • No observed landing URL carried an Associates tag — tag=shopnow047c-20 was absent in every observed target. Commission attribution is therefore unconfirmed (FLG-003).
  • Identifiers embedded in the submitted paths are 9 characters; Amazon's official ASIN definition is 10 [S05], and the observed landing ASINs differ from the submitted path identifiers in every observed case.
  • No verdict is claimed. The links' exact compliance status is unresolved until tested in the enrolled Associates workflow (gate G5) — one link per product, destination and tracking report confirmed before scaling.

Compliance posture

  • Disclosure strip above the fold on every page + required statement in every footer, enforced by CI (compliance gate, standard [S04]/[S07]).
  • Every outbound product link carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener", (paid link) labeling, and a data attribute for click measurement.
  • A kill switch (require_verified_links in products.json) disables all outbound CTAs site-wide if flipped — the gate in front of what can't be automated.
  • No Amazon trademarks, logos or scraped imagery are used anywhere; site imagery is AI-generated and labeled as illustrative.
