About & editorial standards

Who writes this site, what we will and won't publish, and how reader support works.

The publication

ShopNow Kitchen Edit (working brand — FLG-006) is a small, reader-supported publication about air fryers, multi-function ovens, and the tools that keep them honest. It is written and maintained by the editorial team; we don't publish under invented personal bylines, and every guide carries its review date.

The premise is simple: the questions that decide a kitchen purchase are boring and practical — what you'll wash, what fits the counter, whether the presets match your actual week. We publish the questions first, the picks second, and we say so when a listing is still unconfirmed.

Reader support

When you buy through links on this site, we may earn an affiliate commission. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Product links are marked rel="sponsored" and labeled "(paid link)". This never changes what we publish: our records are public, our blanks are visible, and no manufacturer sponsors any page.

Imagery policy

All site imagery is AI-generated illustration — labeled as such, never passed off as product photography. We don't use Amazon product images, logos or marks; those are usable only through licensed formats we don't employ. SG-10 is the open decision on future imagery.

What we will publish

  • Category guidance and comparison frameworks that don't depend on unconfirmed specifics.
  • Product records with dated observations and sourced confirmations.
  • Blank cells, visibly blank, with flags — when a value can't be sourced.
  • Corrections: if a record is wrong, the fix and the date go in the same record.

What we won't publish

  • Prices, ratings, specs or availability we haven't confirmed from an official source.
  • Claims about search popularity, product superiority or performance without a source.
  • Fake urgency ("only 3 left at this price!") — that's manufactured, not reported.
  • Any conversion score or revenue forecast — the ledger stays honest.
Stay in the loop: new guides land in the RSS feed first. The email capture records interest locally until the newsletter decision (SG-11) lands — nothing is sent anywhere yet.
