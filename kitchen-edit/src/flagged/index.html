What we know (observation pass, 2026-11-20)
  • All five URLs are second-level names under the .amazon gTLD, operated by Amazon Registry Services, Inc. [S09].
  • Four resolved to amazon.com product pages; one hit an Amazon checkpoint interstitial (FLG-004).
  • None of the observed landing URLs contained tag=shopnow047c-20; landing URLs carried lv=shuf and btn_ref=srctok-… parameters instead.
  • Identifiers embedded in the submitted paths are 9 characters; Amazon's official ASIN definition is 10 characters [S05]; observed landing ASINs (10 chars) differ from the path identifiers in every observed case.
  • The Operating Agreement pays commission only on purchases following its special "tagged" link formats [S01, S03].
What is missing
  • Whether these are Associates-generated Special Links carrying our tag — determinable only inside the account.
How to discover
  • Associates Central → Reports → Links: click one link and check attribution; regenerate one link per product via SiteStripe/Link Builder; or one GetItems lookup per ASIN once Creators API is active [S13]. Test one link per product; confirm destination AND tracking report before scaling.
No-verdict rule
  • No session may claim the supplied short links are valid or invalid from outside the account. Status stays "unresolved" until gate G5 is performed.
Sources
  • S01 — Operating Agreement
  • S03 — Program Policies / Commission Income Statement
  • S05 — ASIN definition
  • S09 — IANA .amazon entry
