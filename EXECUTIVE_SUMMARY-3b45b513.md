## The one paragraph

A publication now exists that a stranger can read without being told it is a compliance
exercise: a front page led by five appliances, seven question-led guides, a counter-space
checker that answers the question people actually have before they buy, and one footer
group where the working papers live. It is generated from its own register and gated by
4,266 checks that fail the build rather than warn. What it cannot do is earn: none of the
five supplied links carries the tracking tag, and no tagged link has been confirmed in an
Associates report. That gap is not a code problem and it is not hidden.

## The finding that matters most

**No supplied link carries `tag=`.** Program Policies 2(b) requires the Associates ID as a URL
parameter in every link placed on a site, and the Commission Income Statement 2(g)
disqualifies purchases that are not correctly tracked because links are not formatted.
So this edition does not publish the short links at all: it publishes
`amazon.com/dp/<observed asin>?tag=shopnow047c-20`, marked `rel="sponsored noopener"`, and
labels every one provisional because **formatting is not validation** (`CDF-04`, gate G-02).

Precisely what is *not* claimed: no document read here says these short links are invalid.
Their compliance status is unresolved (`CDF-03`), which is a different sentence.

## Two corrections to earlier work in this repository

1. **The ThermoMaven has a manufacturer page.** Previous registers concluded none existed and
   withheld every specification. The listing's model field reads `HT07`; the maker sells that
   model as the F1 Stealth, with a manual stating a −58 to 572 °F range, a 4.3-inch probe and
   a one-year-AAA cell. Those are now published from official sources (`CDF-08`).
2. **"Two different machines" was overstated.** Chefman does sell `RJ38-SQSS-8T`, the model the
   8 Qt listing names. The two official pages still disagree on **wattage** (1000 W vs 1700 W)
   and **dimensions** (12.75 × 13.5 × 10 vs 15.4 × 12.6 × 13.5), so both stay withheld with
   both figures recorded (`CDF-05`, `CDF-09`).

Both corrections are published rather than quietly applied, because a correction log that
deletes its own mistakes cannot be audited.

## The honest position on revenue

It earns **nothing today**, and will earn nothing until a tagged link is confirmed in a report.
No conversion rate, revenue forecast or traffic estimate appears anywhere in this edition,
because none exists. The ledger has nine columns, zero rows and a rule that keeps it that way
until an authorised first-party report arrives. When the first month of data exists, the right
response is to read it — not to scale content against it.
