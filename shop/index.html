What we would tell a friend

If two things cook at once

CHEFMAN 6 Qt. Dual Basket. Two independent 3 qt baskets with a setting that lands them together.

If it is one big pile

Chefman TurboFry 8 Qt. Eight quarts in a single basket, and a burst of heat at the end.

If a whole meal goes in

Emeril Lagasse 26 QT French Door. 26 quarts and two levels, if the counter depth is there.

If you want your hands free

CHEF iQ Sense. A leave-in probe that reports while the lid stays shut.
