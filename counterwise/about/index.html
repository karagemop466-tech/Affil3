Who is speaking

About, and what a byline means here

Counterwise is an editorial system with a verification gate. Pages are signed Counterwise Editorial Desk because that is what they are: assembled from listings that were loaded and from policy documents that were read, then refused or allowed by a build script. There is no test kitchen, and the site says so in place of a stock photo of someone holding a spatula.

The rules we hold ourselves to

  • No claim of use. Nothing is "tested", "tried" or "hands-on" unless someone here has done it, and no one has.
  • No invented attributes. A product string is either lifted from the listing with its capture date, or the field is blank and flagged.
  • No prices, no ratings. Amazon shows the live price at the click.
  • Commission is disclosed above the fold, on every page. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
  • Mistakes get published, not deleted. The review page lists what is unsettled, with the source that would settle it.

Corrections

If a row here is wrong, the fix is a change to data/ in the repository, followed by a rebuild; the site's HTML is generated, so an edit that is not in the register does not survive. That is a deliberate inconvenience: it makes an unsourced change impossible.

Why this niche

Because four of five supplied links resolved to countertop cooking appliances and the fifth to a probe thermometer, which is a coherent shelf rather than a random set of products. Adopting what the evidence said, instead of inventing a market that sounded better, is the entire method in miniature.
