Retrieval notes

IDMethod and the wording relied on
SG-01Page retrieved in full during this session (single chunk, no pagination).

What it says that we rely on: Amazon's help page states that to comply with FTC regulations a link-level disclosure must be Clear (a clear disclosure could be as simple as "(paid link)", "#ad", or "#CommissionsEarned") and Conspicuous (placed near any affiliate link or product review in a location customers will notice easily; they shouldn't have to hunt for it). It further states that the Operating Agreement requires the statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." to appear clearly and conspicuously on the Site.
SG-02The US copy renders only inside a signed-in Associates session. Text retrieved on 2026-11-20 from the official Amazon.sa copy of the same policy document and cross-checked against a 2017 archived copy of the US URL on web.archive.org.

What it says that we rely on: Wording relied on: "you must include your Associates ID or 'tag' (appearing as XXXXX-20, or such other format as we may designate) as a parameter in the URL of each link you place on your Site to an Amazon Site"; "Special Links must link to Products"; "When linking to pages with Product lists you must have additional original content on your Site that is relevant to the Special Link." Re-read the US copy inside the enrolled account before scale: FLG-006.
SG-03Login-gated. Not retrieved in this session; no clause from it is quoted or relied on anywhere on the site.

What it says that we rely on: Listed so the gate can prove the gap instead of hiding it. The site's disclosure text comes from SG-01, which is Amazon's own restatement of the agreement's requirement, so the site is compliant on that point without claiming to have audited the contract. See Q-02.
SG-04Retrieved during this session; Table A read in full, Table B partially (first rows).

What it says that we rely on: Table A as read: "Furniture, Home, Home Improvement, Lawn & Garden, Pets Products — 3.00%" and "Digital Music, Physical Music, Handmade, Digital Videos, Health & Personal Care, Sports, Kitchen, Automotive — 1.25%". A Direct Qualifying Purchase is defined as a Qualifying Purchase of a Product of the same ASIN variant as the Product detail page a customer linked to from the content. Which Table A row an air fryer falls under is not stated on the page: FLG-005.
SG-05Page retrieved in full during this session; last updated 2025-12-10 UTC per the page footer.

What it says that we rely on: Wording relied on: "Mark links that are advertisements or paid placements (commonly called paid links) with the sponsored value." Multiple rel values may be given as a space- or comma-separated list, which is why the build emits rel="sponsored nofollow".
SG-06Text retrieved from the official developers.google.com result on 2026-11-20; the page itself was not opened to the anchor.

What it says that we rely on: Wording relied on: "Scaled content abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating Search rankings and not helping users … no matter how it's created." This is the single most important constraint on an AI-powered affiliate platform: volume is the risk, not the asset.
SG-07Page retrieved during this session.

What it says that we rely on: As read: product structured data has two main classes — Product snippets "for product pages where people can't directly purchase the product", and merchant listings "for pages where customers can purchase products from you". It also says rich presentation can surface price, availability and review ratings, which is data this site deliberately does not publish. Hence no Product or Review JSON-LD on this build: see S-11 and FLG-011.
