What to look at before you decide

  • Two 3-quart baskets is six quarts of volume, not six quarts of one batch — decide whether you actually cook two components at once
  • The title promises a single finish for both baskets; ask what happens when one basket needs four more minutes
  • Twin-basket machines are roughly twice the footprint of a single basket: measure the strip of counter and the cabinet height before ordering
  • View windows and auto shutoff are convenience features, not safety features; do not trade a probe thermometer for either
