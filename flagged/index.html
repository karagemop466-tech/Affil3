FLG-028 high The Site may not have price tracking or price alerting functionality unless Amazon agrees

Participation Requirements §6(y), read verbatim 2026-11-20: "Unless otherwise agreed by Amazon, your Site must not have price tracking and/or price alerting functionality." (S02) The brief asks the platform to "analyze pricing" and to track conversions. Recording prices for internal analysis is a different act from shipping a price-tracking feature to readers, and only the second is barred by 6(y) - but the distinction has to be deliberate, not accidental. Related and stricter: §2(b) allows the Site to show prices and availability only where Amazon serves the link or the data comes from the Creators API or PA API. Neither is in place, which is why the build gate fails on any currency amount in the HTML and why FLG-016 stands.
