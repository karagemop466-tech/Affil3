Flagged for review

What we could not confirm

Irregularities, conflicts between two official sources, and values that could not be confirmed. Each is published on the Flagged for review page with its source links and the manual step that would close it. Flags never block publication; they tell a reader what not to trust yet. Numbering is continuous across sessions, and a resolved flag is kept rather than deleted so the audit trail survives.

Nothing here blocks the site. Each item is a gap between what a reader might assume and what we can actually show. The manual step in each card is the fastest way to close it.

FLG-001 high Submitted tokens are 9 characters, not 10

All five submitted tokens (B04G1MEwY, B02NbDxp3, B00hJ57DV, B0c8moW59, B0f3GKalj) are 9 characters long. The observed amazon.com product identifiers in S04 are all 10 characters, and secondary sources describe ASINs as 10-character identifiers. No Amazon-owned definition was retrieved (see FLG-012). Because the intended ASIN cannot be confirmed, the intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date and confirmation source are left blank for every row.

Sources: S04, S05, S12

What would close it: Confirm the intended product for each submitted link in Associates Central (or in the Amazon product page you intended) and supply the full 10-character ASIN or product URL.

FLG-002 high Observed destination ASIN differs from the token in the link

The short links resolved to different 10-character ASINs than the submitted token (for example B04G1MEwY resolved to B0CDY277D3). The observed destinations are recorded in separate fields and are not treated as the intended product. The site links to the observed destination only as a tagged, observed product (see FLG-005).

Sources: S04, S05

What would close it: Open each submitted short link in a signed-in browser and confirm the product shown. Confirm whether the intended product is the observed product before it is published as the buy link.

FLG-003 high Compliance status of the supplied short links is unresolved

The short links are not claimed to be invalid. Associates Program Policies Participation Requirements (w) restrict a link-shortening service in a manner that makes it unclear that the link goes to an Amazon Site (S02). Whether the link.amazon short links fall within that restriction has not been determined. The site does not use the short links for outbound links; they appear only as source evidence. Added 2026-11-20: Amazon's Associates help (S13) states that Amazon full links and Amazon short links are supported in the OneLink setup, and that third-party shortened links are not. Participation Requirements 2(a) requires the Associates tag as a parameter in each Special Link (S02). None of the four observed product redirects contains a tag parameter (see products). Attribution of the link.amazon links to shopnow047c-20 therefore remains unconfirmed, and this flag stays open.

Sources: S02, S05, S13

What would close it: Check the supplied short links against Associates Program Policies (w) and the Associates tracking reports. Decide whether to keep them as evidence only or to replace them completely.

FLG-005 high Tagged Special Links are not yet validated in Associates Central

The outbound links use the Associates tracking ID shopnow047c-20 in the /dp/ format (?tag=). Operating Agreement §1 defines Special Links as links in the tagged formats Amazon provides, and Participation Requirements §2 requires the tag as a URL parameter (S01, S02). The links have not been generated or tested in Associates Central, and no tracking report has been reviewed, because the sandbox has no Associates login. Gate G-02 requires one link per product to be validated before any scaling.

Sources: S01, S02, S04

What would close it: Sign in to Associates Central, generate one Special Link per observed product with the enrolled tracking ID, click it, confirm the destination and the tracking report, then mark G-02 done.

FLG-026 high The 8 Qt air fryer listing and the manufacturer page describe two different machines

The Amazon listing for B08DKYBTPH states, in its own 'About this item' copy, that the unit is 1000 W/120 V, model RJ38-SQSS-8T, with a 200-400 °F range, a 12.75 x 13.5 x 10 in body and a stainless-steel finish. Chefman's own product page for the 8 Qt model with Hi-Fry describes RJ38-8T-BLACK, 1700 W/120 V, up to 450 °F, and a matte-black finish. Both cannot describe the same appliance. This site therefore publishes capacity (8 qt, which both agree on) and withholds wattage, temperature range, dimensions and finish. The listing's figures are shown in a separate column so a reader can see both without either being presented as verified.

Sources: S16, S12

What would close it: Confirm which model ships from the listing, by checking the model number printed on the unit or the box, or by asking Chefman support whether RJ38-SQSS-8T and RJ38-8T-BLACK share a listing.

FLG-027 medium The same listing carries two different temperature ranges in two places

A Hi-Fry burst and a 400 °F ceiling can coexist if Hi-Fry is a separate short-lived mode, but the listing never explains it. Because the two statements are not reconcilable from the page alone, no temperature figure is published for this product.

Sources: S16

What would close it: The user guide linked from the listing should settle it. Read it and supply the page reference.
