Flagged for review

Irregularities, conflicts between two official sources, and values that could not be confirmed. Each is published on the Flagged for review page with its source links and the manual step that would close it. Flags never block publication; they tell a reader what not to trust yet. Numbering is continuous across sessions, and a resolved flag is kept rather than deleted so the audit trail survives. FLG-031 to FLG-035 were added by session 66f2548a on 2026-11-20 after re-reading the Operating Agreement and the Program Policies verbatim; the numbering continues from main's FLG-030 because this branch's earlier allocations were dropped in two merges rather than renumbered over main's. FLG-035 is recorded as resolved by main's own /privacy/ page, which shipped independently of this session. Flags do not block publication.
