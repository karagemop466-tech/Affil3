Flagged for review

What we could not confirm

Irregularities, conflicts between official sources, and values that could not be confirmed. Each one is published on the Flagged for review page with the source links and the single manual step that would close it. Flags never block publication; they tell a reader what not to trust yet. Numbering restarts here to match this build's source register.

Nothing here blocks the site. Each item is a gap between what a reader might assume and what we can actually show. The manual step in each card is the fastest way to close it.

FLG-001 high Two supplied short links did not resolve to a product, and no link can be treated as the intended product

Five short links were supplied. Fetched three times each on 2026-11-20, four resolved to a product page and one (B0c8moW59) returned an Amazon 'Continue shopping' checkpoint page with no product. In addition, the observed mapping changed between rounds: B00hJ57DV returned the checkpoint page on the first fetch and the Chefman 8 Qt listing on the second and third. A redirect observed in a browser is not the same thing as the product the owner meant to submit, so no submitted token is published as a confirmed product. Observed destinations are kept in their own fields and are never copied into the intended fields.

Sources: S05, S04

What would close it: Open each supplied short link from your own Amazon session and tell us which product you meant for each one. That single message closes FLG-001 and FLG-002.

FLG-002 high Observed destination ASINs differ from the supplied link codes, and the codes are shorter than an ASIN

All five supplied codes are 9 characters. Amazon assigns a 10-character ASIN to products, and the four product pages that resolved carry 10-character ASINs (B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DZFD6R9Z). The supplied codes are therefore link tokens that resolve to a destination, not ASINs themselves. Nothing here is a claim that the links are invalid; their compliance status is unresolved (FLG-001). The site does not republish the short links as buy links.

Sources: S05, S04, S02

What would close it: None required from the reader. Resolved by the same message that closes FLG-001.

FLG-003 medium CHEF iQ battery life: the manufacturer's own pages disagree

The CHEF iQ Sense product page states '70+ Hours of Battery'. The CHEF iQ Sense FAQ on the same domain states that Gen 3 probes hold a charge for 'up to 60 hours'. Both are official CHEF iQ pages, so the site publishes no battery figure at all rather than picking one.

Sources: S14, S15

What would close it: Ask CHEF iQ support which figure applies to the Gen 3 unit sold under B0DZFD6R9Z, or leave the field blank.

FLG-004 medium Chefman 8 Qt: the Amazon listing's model-number field does not match the manufacturer's model for this feature set

The Chefman TurboFry 8 Qt page names model RJ38-8T-BLACK and features four cooking functions plus a 450 F Hi-Fry finish. The Amazon listing for B08DKYBTPH carries the title 'TurboFry 4-in-1', which matches, but its product-information block lists an item model number that does not match the manufacturer's model for this feature set. Two official pages describe the same product differently, so the model number shown on this site comes from the manufacturer only, and the listing field is treated as unreliable.

Sources: S12, S16

What would close it: Confirm the model printed on the unit or the box against RJ38-8T-BLACK before quoting a model number in any ad copy.

FLG-005 medium Emeril Lagasse 26 QT: preset count and accessory count differ across official pages

The Emeril Everyday product page shows '25 Cooking Pre-sets' in its specification strip and '24 one-touch cooking presets' in its own description, and an image on the same page reads '11-in-1'. The Amazon listing title says '24 One-Touch Preset Options' and 'Six Cooking Accessories Included', while the manufacturer description mentions seven. Because the official sources conflict, this site publishes no preset count and no accessory count.

Sources: S13, S04

What would close it: Check the printed quick-start guide in the box, which is the only source that decides it for a shipped unit.

FLG-006 high No Special Link has been tested in Associates Central

Every buy button on this site points at a product page with the tracking tag attached, which matches the tag rule in the Program Policies. None of those links has been clicked inside an enrolled Associates session and confirmed in a tracking report. Testing one link per product is the gate before any additional page is built on top of these links.

Sources: S02, S01

What would close it: In Associates Central, open one product link, confirm the destination, then confirm the click appears in the tracking report. Repeat per product. This closes gate G-02.

FLG-007 medium ThermoMaven: no manufacturer page, so every specification is left blank

The ThermoMaven instant-read thermometer has no manufacturer product page that could be located for this model. The accuracy, certification and waterproofing claims exist only on the marketplace listing. The site therefore shows the product as context with no buy button and no specifications.

Sources: S04

What would close it: If ThermoMaven publishes a product page for this model, send the URL and the specification table can be filled and re-verified.

FLG-008 medium Associates account status and the declared site are unknown

The tracking ID shopnow047c-20 is recorded as supplied by the owner. This build cannot see the Associates account, so it cannot confirm that the account is active, that this site is declared in the account, or that the tracking ID belongs to it.

Sources: S01, S02

What would close it: In Associates Central, confirm the account status and that this GitHub Pages site is listed under account settings.

FLG-009 medium Analytics endpoint is not deployed, so collection is inactive on the live site

The browser script and the collector are written and tested, but GitHub Pages is static and cannot run the collector. Until an endpoint is configured in data/config.json and the collector is deployed somewhere that can run Node, the click counter sends nothing. No traffic or conversion figure on this site is measured.

What would close it: Deploy tools/collector.mjs to a host that runs Node, then set analyticsEndpoint in data/config.json. Nothing else needs to change.

FLG-010 low Search Console is not connected

The site publishes a sitemap and a feed, but no Search Console property has been verified, so no query data exists. The next topic cannot be chosen from measured queries until that changes.

What would close it: Verify the property in Search Console and submit the sitemap URL listed in robots.txt.

FLG-011 low Publisher byline is a team name, not a named person

Pages are published under 'Affil3 editorial team'. Google's guidance and the FTC both favour a clear statement of who is responsible for the content. A named editor with a real contact route would be stronger, but inventing one would be worse.

Sources: S09

What would close it: Supply the name and role of the person who will answer for corrections, and it will be added to the About page.

FLG-012 low Two publications exist in the repository

An earlier session built a second publication under /editorial/ with a different masthead and its own data. It is reachable from the footer of this site and is preserved rather than deleted. Its product-to-link mapping is not consistent with the three fetch rounds recorded here, so it is clearly marked as an earlier edition and does not carry current buy links. One site should be indexed; consolidation is offered as a decision on the Suggestions page.

What would close it: Choose option S-01 on the Suggestions page to retire or merge the earlier edition.

FLG-013 medium Product imagery is illustration, not photography of the reviewed units

The photographs on this site are generated editorial images. They contain no brand marks and none of them is a picture of the actual unit being described. They are used for atmosphere only and are labelled as generated in the footer and on the About page.

Sources: S02

What would close it: If real product photography is required, it must come from the manufacturer or Amazon's own image service under the Associates license.

FLG-014 low No price or star rating is published, by policy

Amazon's Program Policies allow a site to show price and availability only if Amazon serves that data or it comes from the Creators or PA API. This site does neither, so it shows no price and no rating, and the buy buttons read 'Check current price on Amazon'. Prices seen during research are deliberately not republished.

Sources: S03

What would close it: If prices are wanted, enroll in the Creators API and serve them through Amazon.
