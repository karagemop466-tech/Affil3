FLG-028 medium 'NIST-certified' on P04 is not supported by a certificate we could find

ThermoMaven describes the F1 Ace as NIST-certified with +/-0.5 °F accuracy. NIST's own published FAQ states that NIST 'does not recommend or endorse commercial equipment, instruments, or materials' and does not 'define, specify, assure, or certify traceability' of measurements. That does not make the accuracy claim false; it means the accuracy has no verifiable certificate behind it in public. The product page quotes the accuracy as the maker's claim and does not repeat 'NIST-certified' as a fact.

Sources: S17, S19

Manual step: Ask ThermoMaven for the calibration certificate or the accredited laboratory and certificate number. If one exists, cite it; otherwise keep the claim labelled as the maker's.

This is a permanent constraint on how persuasive the site can be until a tester is available.

Sources: S07

Manual step: Owner answers Q-14 (which products, if any, have been handled). Pages are then labelled 'tested', 'owned' or 'not tested' per product, and only tested products may carry hands-on claims.
